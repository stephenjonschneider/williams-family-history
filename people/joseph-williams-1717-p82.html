What the records say

In 1738 "Joseph Williams of Freehold, yeoman" joined Andrew Layton and others in a bond to his brother John Williams (W-0090). In 1740 he helped administer the estate of his father-in-law, Thomas Layton of Lower Freehold, whose daughter Sarah he had married (W-0059). By 1743 he held land in Upper Freehold beside Thomas Leonard (W-0240), on Doctors Creek, bought from the Dockwra proprietors (W-0189). He died without a will in 1746. His inventory was taken on 23 August; his widow Sarah gave up the administration, and his brother John took it on 26 September — the day after John took administration of their father, Thomas Williams (G11), as his son (W-0061, W-0062). Surveys from 1750 to 1801 still describe "a tract formerly belonging to Joseph Williams deceased" on Doctors Creek (W-0025, W-0185). No record names his children. In May 1751 his widow Sarah, signing by mark, consented to their daughter Mary's marriage to Richard Hulitt [W-0477]; two more Freehold Williams girls, Marah and Sarah, married Samuel Hays (1754) and Samuel Layton (1755) [W-0478, W-0241].
