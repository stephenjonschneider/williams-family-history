[superseded round 164: "Probable — ... a Grover Family typescript lists him first ..."]

What the records say

His gravestone, photographed on an online family tree, reads "[In Memory of] Safety Taylor who departed this Life November [..] 1766 Aged 4[5?] Years [..] Months and [..] Days", with a verse below; the tree gives him the dates 1714-1766 (BG-0695). A typescript "Grover Family" genealogy lists Safety Taylor first among the nine children of William Taylor, son of Edward Taylor of Middletown, and Hannah, daughter of Safety Grover; it cites William's will, proved 12 Oct 1767, which names his wife Hannah and his brother-in-law James Grover (BG-0696). A 1939 genealogy query and Stillwell likewise marry a daughter of Safety Grover to a Taylor (BG-0501, BG-0516).
