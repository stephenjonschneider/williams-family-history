What the records say

Samuel Bowne the tailor signed himself "Samuel Bowne Talor" as surety for John Wall and Rachel Bowne in April 1751 (BG-0657), and "Sam. Bowne of Middletown, Taylor" on his own marriage bond with Patience Covenover, 3 Sept 1754 (BG-0672). Stillwell says he received Middletown land in 1750 and 1754 "from his father, Samuel Bowne, and Esther Bowne", the second beside his brother Safety, and mortgaged it in 1776 (BG-0519); the Holland Society also calls him a son of Samuel and Esther and lists twelve children born 1755-1778 (BG-0658). He appraised John Cooper's estate with Safety Bowne in 1762 (BG-0659) and arbitrated with Safety in 1767 (BG-0061). His gravestone gives death 11 March 179[6/9] aged 77 (BG-0661).
