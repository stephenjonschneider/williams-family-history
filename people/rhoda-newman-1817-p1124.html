What the records say

Rhoda, wife of John Allen, is one of the six heirs named in the December 1847 notice and the 1848 Orphans Court division of the Cross Branch farm of her father, Joseph I. Newman (G8) (N-0044, N-0016, N-0014). Lot 2, of 33 acres, was set off to her, and on 25 August 1848 John L. Allen and his wife Rhoda sold it to Samuel Morris Jr, reserving one chain square 'where the burying ground now is' (N-0018, N-0289). A 1909 sheriff's sale still describes the neighbouring land as running to 'the lot set off for Rhoda Allen, formerly Rhoda Newman' (N-0048). The compiler reports that her death certificate names Lydia as her mother; that certificate has not yet been abstracted.
