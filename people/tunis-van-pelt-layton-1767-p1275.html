What the records say

In Mar 1788 Tunis Layton was present at Aaron Layton's survey as owner of the adjoining land, and he carried the chain on neighbouring surveys in 1788–89 (L-0308, L-0313, L-0314). In 1789 Kenneth Hankinson, agent of the General Proprietors, sued him for £100, the sheriff returning that he could not be found (L-0426). His father William's (G9) will of 1793 names him (L-0057), and in 1795 he had a tract surveyed by the Gravil or Stone Branch (L-0299). In Apr 1802 he and his wife Elenor quitclaimed to his brother William Jr (L-0076), and in May 1802 his parents deeded him 5.2 a. (L-0077). In 1811 he was an heir of Hester Cooper (L-0088). He bought North Branch land in 1815 and 1819 and exchanged land with Jesse Emmons in 1816 (L-0111, L-0116, L-0117, L-0149). Stillwell gives his will of 10 July 1832, proved 25 Aug 1832 (L-0404), and the 1873 death return of his son Aaron names Tunis as father (L-0402).
