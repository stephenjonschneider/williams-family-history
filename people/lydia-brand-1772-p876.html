What the records say

A Lydia Newman is named as a legatee in Thomas Brand Sr's will of 2 April 1813 and in the 1814 division of his land (N-0085, N-0041). Lydia, widow of Joseph P. Newman, was granted administration of his estate on 25 May 1841 (N-0205), received her dower in 1843–44 on the road from the Long Causeway to the sea (N-0102, N-0103), and settled the estate account in 1844 (N-0104). The 1850 census lists Lydia Newman, 78, in Howell (N-0220). She died without a will; letters went to Jesse B. Newman on 14 July 1857 (N-0222).
