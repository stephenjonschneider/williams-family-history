What the records say

Safety Bowne married Phebe Worden; the WPA card for the bond (B-16) gives 22 Apr 1743, witnesses Anna and William Madock, and SAMUEL BOWNE as bondsman (BG-0675); the official index has 22 Apr 1742 (BG-0545). He was a Monmouth freeholder in 1748 (BG-0656) and appraised John Cooper's estate with Samuel Bowne in 1762 (BG-0659). His land bounded the parcel his parents Samuel (G11?) and Esther (G11?) gave his brother Samuel in 1754 (BG-0519). On 13 Nov 1767 "Safety and Samuel Bowne, brothers, sons of Samuel Bowne, deceased" agreed to arbitration (BG-0061). In 1773 he and Phebe sold Anthony Layton land by Samuel Bowne's line and the Safety Grover/John Bowne corner (BG-0066).
