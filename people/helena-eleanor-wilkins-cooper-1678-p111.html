What the records say

Helena Wilkins married Benjamin Cooper (G11?) in New York on 7 April 1694, according to a compiled index of marriages before 1699 (C-0307). The Staten Island list of inhabitants of about 1706 names Benjamin Coper, Elener Coper and a girl, Martha Coper; Elener is very probably Helena (C-0310). An online memorial for Thomas Cooper (G10?) names his mother as "Helena Elenor Cooper" (C-0333). No record of her death has been found; by 1754 Benjamin had married Hester Borden.
