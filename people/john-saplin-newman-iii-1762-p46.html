What the records say

The Bible of his son Jeremiah Newman Jr records 'John Newmon, my father', born 17 July 1762, died 26 March 1830 aged 68, and 'Rebecca Newmon, my mother', born 1751, died 1809 (N-0199). As John Newman Jr he was present at Shark River surveys in 1791 and 1795 beside an older John (N-0073, N-0077), and his uncle Emanuel's 1795 will names him among the sons of Emanuel's brother John (N-0212). He died without a will; letters went to his son John Saplin Newman on 6 April 1830 (N-0106, N-0110). In 1836 the Orphans Court named fifteen children and heirs, among them Jeremiah Jr, Joseph, John Saplin, Aaron and Francis, some still minors (N-0107); Francis's objection stayed the division (N-0108, N-0109). The heirs later traded their eighths of his farm (N-0232, N-0234, N-0237).
