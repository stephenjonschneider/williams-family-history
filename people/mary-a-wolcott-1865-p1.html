What the records say

Mary Ann's death certificate gives her birth on 21 November 1865 at Smithburg and names her parents, Amos A. Wolcott (G6) and Elizabeth Ann Clevenger (G6); her husband Thomas E. Williams (G5) was the informant (WO-0001). A printed family marriage certificate records her marriage to Thomas, as Mary A. Wolcott of Monmouth County, in December of a year in the 1880s that is no longer legible; one witness was a Sarah (WO-0002). Census records held in the Williams workbook show her in Howell in 1900 and 1910 and on Ridge Avenue, Lakewood, in 1930; her son Harry F. Williams's birth certificate names his mother as Mary Wolcott. She died on 10 March 1944 at her home on Lakewood Road, Point Pleasant, after twelve years there, and was buried at Woodlawn Cemetery, Lakewood (WO-0001). A family tree adds her brother John Caleb Butler Clevenger (WO-0015), and an annotated map marks Smithburg as her birthplace (WO-0080).
