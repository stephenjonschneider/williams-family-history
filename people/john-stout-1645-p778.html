What the records say

John Stout had a headright "for himselfe and his wife" in 1675 (BG-0426) and a house-lot survey in Middletown in 1678 (BG-0387). He built up land in the town and the Poplar Field, buying from Richard Sadler in 1686 and 1696 and from James Grover in 1697 (BG-0437, BG-0245, BG-0423), and in 1688 bought a share of John Throckmorton's propriety (BG-0444). As "farmer" he sold a lot to John Bowne in 1689 (BG-0445). Named in his father Richard Stout's (G13?) will of 1703 (BG-0310), he released the Hop River homestead to his brother Benjamin on 5 Oct 1705, calling Richard "my late father deceased" (BG-0231). By 1714 he was "Capt. John Stout, gent." (BG-0203), and his son Richard was his heir at law by 1742 (BG-0241).
