What the records say

The 1850 census lists Charles, 15, at school in his father Henry H. Wolcott's (G7) household at Millstone (WO-0083). His father's will names him a son and co-executor with his brother Amos A. (G6) (WO-0060), and in 1876 his sisters gave the executors receipts for their legacies (WO-0142). The 1873 county map marks his dwelling, store and hotel at Charleston Springs (WO-0079). A 1905 county biography gives his birth on 10 July 1834 and tells of the Wolcott Hotel at Freehold and his grandfather Asher Morse's distillery (WO-0081). His mother died at his Freehold home in 1893 (WO-0111), and Amos's 1908 obituaries name him as Amos's brother (WO-0076, WO-0077). By 1866–67 he kept a saloon at Freehold, where he had endorsed notes for Garret Schanck and was listed among Schanck's creditors in bankruptcy (WO-0176).
