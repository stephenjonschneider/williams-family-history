What the records say

Catherine (G10) is known chiefly through her husband John Layton (G10). His will of 1 Mar 1761 tells his sons to provide for "their mother" and names their children Safety, Andrew, John, William (G9), Thomas, Catharine, Hester, Hannah and Ann (BG-0057). After his death she headed her own Shrewsbury household in the Revolutionary rosters of 1777 (BG-0069), the tax ratables of 1779-81 (BG-0071) and the Shrewsbury tax of April 1782 (BG-0679). Stillwell calls her "Miss Bowne" (BG-0086, BG-0305). The Descendants of Founders application has her dying 13 June 1807, but no record supports it: the Monmouth probate index has no estate for her, and the Catherine Layton of 1807 records is the widow of her son William (G9), remarried as Catherine Clayton (BG-0739, BG-0740). Her uncle James Grover's will of 1780 does not name her (BG-0678).
