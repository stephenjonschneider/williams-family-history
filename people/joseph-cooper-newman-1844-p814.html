What the records say

The 1850 census lists Joseph Newman, 6, at school, in the Howell household of Silas Newman (G7) and Elizabeth (G7) (N-0220). In the 1865 state census a Joseph E. Newman lived with the widowed Elizabeth Newman, next to his sister Catherine Louisa (G6), with Silas F. and Mary E. (N-0273). The family tree calls him Joseph Cooper Newman, after his mother's family.
