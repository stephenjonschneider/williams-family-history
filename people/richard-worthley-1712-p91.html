What the records say

Richard Worthley of Shrewsbury was born in the eighth month of 1712, a son of John Worthley and Elizabeth Hance (WO-0180). He married first, in 1742, Elizabeth Williams, by whom he had John, Elizabeth (later Corlies) and Daniel, and second, in 1747, Mary White, daughter of Thomas and Elizabeth (Borden) White; Lydia, born October 1749, was Mary's child (WO-0180). His will of 24 February 1784 left the land to his grandson Richard because his son John was 'absent' for his conduct in the war, and gave half of some land to his 'daughter, Lydia, wife of John Borden'. John and Joel Borden witnessed, and his brothers-in-law Thomas White and Nathan Woolley were executors with his wife Mary. The inventory was taken on 21 June 1784 and the will proved in 1785 (WO-0186).
