What the records say

On 5 Mar 1730 Samuel Layton (G11?) gave his son Andrew the Osborne tract near the head of the Manasquan and the 100 a. Drummond survey with a small propriety right (L-0041, L-0042); the next day Andrew Layton of Freehold, yeoman, sold the north 30 a. of the Osborne tract to David Clayton Jr (L-0043). On 30 May 1733, as heir at law of Samuel Layton deceased and with the consent of 'Jannet his mother', he released shares of the Great Meadow land to his brothers Samuel Jr and Thomas Jr (L-0044, L-0045), and in 1735 they conveyed them back to him (L-0046). In 1738 he was one of six Freehold men bound together with Joseph Williams and Christopher Romine Jr (L-0374). An Andrew Layton sued by Gawen Drummond in 1763 may be him (L-0334).
