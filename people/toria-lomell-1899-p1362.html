Probable — George Lomell (G5, P50) & Pauline Peterson (G5, P51): the 1900 census lists her as their daughter (W-0431).

What the records say

The only record of Toria is the 1900 census of Falmouth, Massachusetts, which lists her as the infant daughter of George and Pauline Lomell, born in September 1899 and less than a year old (W-0431). In 1910 Pauline reported four children born and three living, so Toria is understood to have died in infancy (W-0425). The 1900 page's birth dates are not exact, and her sister Ruth (G4), born in February 1900, is not listed on it. No baptism, death or burial record for her has been found.
