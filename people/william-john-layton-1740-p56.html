What the records say

John Layton's (G10) will of 1 Mar 1761 names William as a son and an executor (L-0054). He and Catherine Van Pelt married by licence of 19 Nov 1764, his brother Safety his bondsman (L-0490, L-0414). In 1769, as William Layton of Shrewsbury, carpenter, he mortgaged 100 a. by the Mill Brook and the tract on the south side of the North Branch of the Metedeconk to Isaac Vandorn (L-0224, L-0225), and as 'William Layton Jun. (son of John)' gave Edmund Williams a warrant of attorney (L-0338). In 1785 Andrew Layton sued him as 'William Layton son of John Layton carpenter' (L-0421). His will of 16 Mar 1793, signed by mark, names his wife Catherine and children Aaron, Tunis, John, Thomas (G8), Walter, William, Mary Britton and Catherine (L-0057). In 1802 he and Catharine, of Howell, deeded 5.2 a. to Tunis (L-0077). The will was proved 26 Feb 1807 (L-0057), and his heirs released their shares to Aaron Layton Jr in 1816 (L-0113–L-0115, L-0121). DFNJ's birth year, 1744, fits the 1761 will.
