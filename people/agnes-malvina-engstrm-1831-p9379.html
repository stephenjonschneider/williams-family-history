What the records say

Agnes Malvina Engström is named as the mother of Johan Gustaf Georg — George Lomell — in the Alingsås town parish birth register: he was born on 4 March 1868, and she is entered as 'Mamsell', aged 37, living at house no. 137 [W-0631]. 'Mamsell' was the title of an unmarried woman, and no father is entered. George's 1892 marriage record at Malden names his mother as Agnes [W-0629], and the compiler's family tree gives her as Agnes Malvina, born 1831 and died 1923, wife of Nikolaus August Lomell [W-0630]. Whether she later married Lomell, and where she died, are not yet known; the Alingsås household examination rolls are the next place to look.
