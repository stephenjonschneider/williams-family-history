What the records say

Safety Borden of Bordentown, making his will on 18 Nov 1757, left "my grandson, Safty Meghee" an acre of land joining Thomas Gridges and £50 after his grandmother's death, charging him to care for his aunt Hannah Borden; another grandson, James Meghee, received a lot (BG-0560). Stillwell's Grover account lists Safety and James among the children of William Maghee of Allentown and his wife Amy Borden, Safety Borden's daughter (BG-0531); William McGee, a brewer of Allentown, was dead by 13 Aug 1744, when his widow Amy swore to his inventory (BG-0561). Safety Meghee was one of the constituent members of the Baptist church of Upper Freehold on 13 May 1766, beside a Mary Meghee (BG-0694); the rare name makes him almost certainly the grandson of 1757.
