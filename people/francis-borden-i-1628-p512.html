What the records say

Francis Borden of Shrewsbury made his will on 24 May 1703, naming his wife Jane, his sons Richard, Thomas and Francis, his daughter Joyce, wife of John Hance Jr, and his 'brother John Borden'; it mentions an inheritance from the Fowle family of Cranbrook, Kent, and land he had bought of Edward Williams in 1696, and was proved on 9 May 1706 (WO-0183). He was christened at Cranbrook, Kent, in December 1628 as the son of Richard Borden (WO-0258), was a freeman of Portsmouth, Rhode Island, with his father in 1648 (WO-0259), and received his father's 'land in New Jersey' by the will the Portsmouth Town Council made in 1671 (WO-0260); in 1692 his nephew released to 'my uncle Francis Borden, of Shrewsbury' all rights in the Shrewsbury lands (WO-0261). He was a brother of Benjamin Borden who married Abigail Grover (WO-0177). If his son Francis was the father of John Borden Sr, he is a great-great-grandfather of Lydia Borden (G8), wife of Amos Wolcott.
