What the records say

Justice Goodenough's return records Elizabeth Layton of Freehold marrying Daniel Williams (G7) on 18 Mar 1826, the same day James T. Layton married Sarah Magill (L-0263). She appears in Daniel's Howell household in the 1830 and 1840 censuses (L-0287, L-0288). After Daniel died intestate on 26 May 1843 she and Israel Reynolds took letters of administration (L-0265), and in 1849 her daughter Mary Reynolds petitioned to divide the land among the nine children, Daniel D. (G6) among them (L-0290). In 1850 she lived in Howell with her second husband Gilbert Layton and their infant Elizabeth (L-0292), and Gilbert bought the house lot from commissioners who included Silas Newman (G7, Newman side) (L-0397). In 1852 she and Gilbert, both by mark, released Lot 5 to James E. Cooper (L-0400). The 1865 and 1870 censuses show them together (L-0418, L-0419). Her death certificate records her death at Howell on 25 Mar 1881, aged 76, and her burial at Bethel (L-0221); the DFNJ application's 23 Mar is not preferred. The 1850 census implies a birth c. 1807; the death certificate implies c. 1805.
