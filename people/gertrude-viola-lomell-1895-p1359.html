What the records say

A Brooklyn church recorded the baptism of Gertrud Viola Lomell on 26 January 1903, born 3 October 1895 at Malden, Massachusetts, to George and Paulina Lomell (W-0429). She appears in her parents' household at Falmouth in 1900 and in Brooklyn in 1910 (W-0431, W-0425). In 1919 she married Edward Williams, brother of Harry F. Williams (G4) (W-0424), and her mother's 1948 death notice calls her Mrs Edward Williams (W-0427). Her obituary records her death at 92 in Southern Ocean County Hospital, Manahawkin, after 18 years as a postal clerk in Point Pleasant, and names her husband Edward A., her brother Leonard Lomell and her sister Ruth Williams (G4) (W-0430). The obituary gives Falmouth as her birthplace; the baptism's Malden is preferred.
