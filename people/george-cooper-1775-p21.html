What the records say

Only one record so far names this George Cooper. The 1882 death certificate of his daughter, Mary Elizabeth (Cooper) Newman (G7), gives her father as George Cooper, born in the United States, and her mother as Elizabeth Cooper (G8) (C-0087). The certificate says Mary Elizabeth was born in Monmouth County and lived in New Jersey all her life; her gravestone gives her birth as 18 June 1813 (C-0405), so George was alive and married by about September 1812. No deed, will, census or marriage has yet been found that names him with his wife Elizabeth. Two other men of the name are being tested against him: George Cooper of Howell, whose wife was Anne (P24), and a George Cooper at law in Monmouth in 1821–1832 (P55).
