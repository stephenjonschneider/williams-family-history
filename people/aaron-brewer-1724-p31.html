Parents

Jan Brouwer & Helena (Hilletje) Van Cleef (proof: Compiled)

Compiled — Jan Brouwer (G10, P10) & Hilletje (Helena) Van Cleef (G10, P11): no baptism names Aaron. He is placed by the compiled Brouwer genealogy (BR-0007, BR-0134) and by circumstance. Jan & Hilletje were the only Jan Brouwer couple baptizing children at the Freehold-Middletown church when Aaron was born (unnamed children 21 Jun 1724 and 11 Apr 1726). Aaron's first daughter was Helena and his son John, and his own name Aris came from his probable great-grandfather Aris Van der Bilt. Hendrick (P26) and Benjamin (P27), baptized as Jan & Hilletje's sons in 1735 and 1738, are his brothers in the tree (BR-0249). Rival: an unnamed son of Hans Brouwer, 1723 (Q26).

What the records say

A compiled Brouwer family genealogy records Aaron and Neeltje Cooper at the baptism of their son John in the Freehold and Middletown Dutch Reformed congregations in April 1764 (BR-0007). In 1784 and 1786 Aaron mortgaged his plantation between Long Brook and the Manasquan, "whereon said Aaron Brewer now dwells" (BR-0009, BR-0014), and in 1785 James Davis sued him for debt (BR-0011). His wife Ellenor Neeltje died in December 1785 and is buried at Adelphia (BR-0012). By 1792 he had a second wife, Martha, when they sold half of Great Shopsunk to John Reynolds (BR-0019). On 1 March 1800 Aaron and Martha sold Peter Brewer six acres between the Methodist meeting-house lot and Long Brook, with Mary Williams as a witness, and acknowledged the deed on 28 March (BR-0075). His will of 7 April 1800 provides for Martha and names his daughter Mary Williams (G8); his inventory was taken on 22 April (BR-0022). In 1801 his son John gave the Methodist society its burying ground on the family land (BR-0024). At the Freehold and Middletown Dutch congregations, "Aris Brouwer and Neeltje Couper" had Helena baptized on 5 December 1756, Femmitie in 1758, Antje ("Aaron Brouwer") in 1760, Maria in 1762 and Johannes on 30 April 1764, and "Aron Brewer" married Martha Stilwell there on 3 August 1786 [BR-0257, BR-0258].
