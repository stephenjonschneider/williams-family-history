What the records say

The 1850 census lists Stewart, 15, in the Howell household of Silas Newman (G7) and Elizabeth (G7) (N-0220). He served as a corporal in Co. K, 29th New Jersey Infantry, in 1862–63, in the same company as Daniel D. Williams (G6) (N-0215, N-0268). He married Daniel D.'s sister Josephine Williams (N-0268), and the 1865 state census shows 'Stout' Newman with Josephus and Alfetta near Catherine Louisa (G6) (N-0273). The Howell return of deaths records Stuart Newman, carpenter, son of Silas Newman, dying of typhoid in March 1873 (N-0269); his gravestone at Southard gives 5 March 1873 (N-0215). In 1880 his widow Josephine lived next to Catherine Louisa with Alfredetta, Pembrooke M. and Austin P. (N-0275). The death return's age of 27 does not fit his age of 15 in 1850. With his mother he administered his father's estate from 1859, passing the account in 1862 (N-0297, N-0298).
