What the records say

The 1880 census of Neptune Township lists Andrew, 16, at school, as son in the household of Samuel Bennett, 75, farmer, and his wife Mary, 40, with his sister Lottie, 6 (S-0144); Samuel Bennet had married Mary Layton in 1847 (S-0143). Andrew S. Bennett of Allenwood is named as the father of Safety Bennett, with Malissa V. Bennett as the mother, on Safety's marriage return of 25 Dec 1914 (S-0141), and the newspaper notice names his daughter Mrs. William I. Lane of West Grove (S-0138). He died, a widower and a farmer and handyman at the Allaire Inn, at Monmouth Memorial Hospital, Long Branch, on 26 Mar 1923; the certificate names his parents Sam Bennett and Mary and gives his birth as Aug 1871 at Neptune City (S-0142).
