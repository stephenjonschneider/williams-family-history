What the records say

Albert Layton married Lydia Straughn at Salem, New Jersey, on 7 September 1824 [S-0234]. In 1827 an Albert Layton won a ploughing premium in Gloucester County [S-0232], and in 1830 an Albert Layton household lived beside Edmund Layton in a West Jersey township [S-0233]. By 1843 the family was at Middletown, St. Georges Hundred, Delaware, where their daughter Anna Jane was born [S-0237]; on 21 December 1847 Albert, "of St. Georges Hundred", bought a 328-acre farm in Duck Creek Hundred, Kent County, from the Rev. Pennell Coombe for $4,000 [S-0235]. In 1850 he was a 47-year-old farmer there with Lydia Ann and children Rachel, Thomas, Safty, Albert A., Lydia A., Nathan, Margaret E. and Ann Jane B. [S-0222]. On 1 May 1852 Albert and Lydia A. sold 154 acres of the farm to David D. Barber of Penns Grove, New Jersey, for $3,000 [S-0241], and in March 1853 he bought an acre and a half on the Kenton road [S-0242]. An 1899 deed by the heirs of his eldest daughter, Rachel S. (Layton) Betz Cleaver, names her surviving sisters Lydia Ann Cleaver and Anna J. Schenberger and the children of her dead brothers and sisters in Wilmington, South Carolina and Kansas [S-0244].
