What the records say

Sarah Bowne was born at Gravesend on Long Island on 27 Nov 1669, a daughter of Capt. John Bowne, the Monmouth patentee, and Lydia Holmes, and grew up in Middletown after the family moved to New Jersey. She married Richard Salter, a lawyer and judge of Monmouth County. In 1716 her brother John Bowne, the merchant of Matawan, named his "sister Sarah Saltar" in his will, and also remembered her daughter Hannah Lincoln (BG-0296). Through Hannah she was the great-great-great-grandmother of President Abraham Lincoln (BG-0735). Her death date is not known; the "1717" in online trees is unsupported.
