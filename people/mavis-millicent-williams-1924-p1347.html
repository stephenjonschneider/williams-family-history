What the records say

Mavis's birth certificate records her birth in Point Pleasant on 4 December 1924 to Harry F. Williams (G4) and Ruth Lomell (G4) (W-0405). The 1930 census shows her, aged five, in her grandfather Thomas E. Williams's (G5) Lakewood household with her parents and sister Amada (W-0406). On 14 October 1942 she married Michael P. DiCorcia (G3) at Elkton, Maryland (W-0409), and the 1950 census finds the couple in their own Point Pleasant Beach household (W-0411). Her mother lived with her in Point Pleasant Beach until 1993 (W-0423), and Michael's 1987 obituary names her as his wife, the former Mavis Williams (W-0437). She died on 5 November 1997 at Point Pleasant Hospital; her obituary recalls her work at Jersey Mike's, her flying and her parish of St. Peter's (W-0418, W-0419).
