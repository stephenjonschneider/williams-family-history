What the records say

Benjamin Stout witnessed a Middletown deed in 1701 (BG-0244). His father Richard Stout's (G13?) will of 9 June 1703 names him (BG-0310), and on 5 Oct 1705 his brother John released to him the Hop River homestead tract called Singinnewanus (BG-0231). He witnessed again in 1708 (BG-0222) and held meadow beside Thomas Stillwell's in 1712 (BG-0248). By Oct 1716 deeds speak of "the late Benjamin Stout" (BG-0233); the Stout family history says he moved to New Castle, Delaware, and died in 1734 (BG-0308).
