What the records say

On 28 Nov 1751 Samuel Bowne 'minor' of Middletown and Christopher Morris of the same place gave the governor a £500 bond for Samuel's licence to marry Mary Layton of Middletown, spinster; Thomas Bartow witnessed (L-0480, L-0446, L-0455). The Bowne genealogy shows that this Samuel was the son of Samuel and Esther Bowne and that he married Patience Conover in 1754, so Mary died between 1751 and 1754 (L-0447). Christopher Morris had married Rebecca Layton by licence of May 1742, with Samuel Layton standing surety (L-0455, L-0404), which makes Mary and Rebecca probably sisters; her widower, Samuel Bowne the tailor, remarried Patience Conover by licence of 3 Sept 1754 (L-0492), and in Dec 1755 he stood surety for Samuel Layton of Freehold (L-0482). Stillwell places both women in Samuel's line; the tree puts them under Andrew #4.
