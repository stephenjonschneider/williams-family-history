What the records say

Thomas Lincoln was born in Rockingham County, Virginia, on 6 Jan 1778 and as a boy saw his father killed in Kentucky in 1786. He became a carpenter and farmer, married Nancy Hanks on 12 June 1806, and was the father of Sarah, Abraham and Thomas Jr. In 1816 he moved the family to Indiana, where Nancy died in 1818; he then married Sarah Bush Johnston and in 1830 moved to Illinois, dying in Coles County on 17 Jan 1851 (BG-0735).
