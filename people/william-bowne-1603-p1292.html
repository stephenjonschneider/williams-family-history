What the records say

Compiled histories place William Bowne (G13?) at Salem by the 1630s, with 40 acres at Jefferies Creek in 1636 and the baptisms of his sons James (G12?) in 1636 and Andrew in 1638; they name his wife Ann (G13?) as the boys' mother (BG-0307, BG-0298, BG-0292). He moved to Gravesend with Lady Moody's settlers, was allotted land there in 1649 and sat as a magistrate in 1657 (BG-0291). He drew a lot on Portland Point in Dec 1667 (BG-0170), recorded his earmark there in 1669 (BG-0161), and paid toward the Monmouth purchase in 1670 (BG-0129). He died in 1677, and administration went to his eldest son John "with consent of the rest of his brothers" (BG-0291). Historic Homes gives him a second wife, Mary Haverlads, in 1664, which no record held confirms (BG-0298).
