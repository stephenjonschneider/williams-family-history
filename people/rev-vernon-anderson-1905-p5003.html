What the records say

On Saturday 27 October 1923 Vernon Anderson of Point Pleasant married Sadie Newman of Wall at the Belford Methodist parsonage (N-0283). The 1940 census shows him as a painter of 34 in Point Pleasant with Sadie and their children Alice, Betty, Eileen, Paul and Ruth (N-0098). In 1947 he and Sadie lived at Arnold Avenue, Point Pleasant Beach, when their daughter Eileen married Richard Harris (N-0099), and in 1950 he was a painting contractor of 45 at Point Pleasant Beach (N-0285). His 1979 obituary describes him as a retired Assemblies of God minister, aged 73, of Cedar Glen West, survived by Sadie, his son Paul, four daughters and a sister, Mildred Timberman (N-0100).
