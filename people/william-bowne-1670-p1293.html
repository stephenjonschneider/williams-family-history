What the records say

William Bowne, mariner, made his will on 27 Apr 1706, leaving his land at Crosswicks to his wife Rebecca as executrix and her own land to the child she was expecting; it was proved 14 Mar 1716/17 (BG-0297). Rebecca was one of the six daughters of Joseph Grover for whom land was surveyed about 1698 (BG-0424). Their posthumous daughter Hannah Bowne, under age, was represented by her cousin James Grover in a 1711 suit over the Crosswicks lands (BG-0530). By 30 Dec 1720 all three were dead: William's brother James, "heir at law to his brother William", released the Crosswicks land to Rebecca's brother and heir James Grover (BG-0200).
