What the records say

Elizabeth is known from a single record: the 1882 death certificate of her daughter, Mary Elizabeth (Cooper) Newman (G7), which names the mother as Elizabeth Cooper, born in the United States, and the father as George Cooper (G8) (C-0087). Mary Elizabeth was born on 18 June 1813 in Monmouth County (C-0405, C-0087). The certificate's "Elizabeth Cooper" is word for word the name the daughter herself went by all her life (marriage 1831, census 1850, gravestone), so the informant may have repeated the daughter's own name (C-0406, C-0383, C-0405). No record of Elizabeth's marriage, maiden name, death or burial has yet been found.
