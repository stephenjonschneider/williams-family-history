What the records say

Pasqualina, called Pauline, was born in Italy in April 1855 [W-0548] and married Francesco DiCorcia about 1881; of her eleven children, seven were living in 1910 [W-0547]. She followed her husband to Jersey City about 1901. The family tree gives her maiden name as Digeronimo and her death as 1916 [W-0554], and a Pasqualine DiCorcia is indexed among Jersey City deaths in December 1916 [W-0551] — although the 1920 census still lists a wife Pauline with Frank [W-0540], a conflict still to be settled.
