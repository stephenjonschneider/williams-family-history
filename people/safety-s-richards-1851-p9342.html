What the records say

In 1860 Safety Richards, 10, was the youngest son of Ephraim Richards, a Maryland-born farmer of St. Albans Township, Licking County, Ohio, and his Ohio-born wife Margaret, with Rebecca Richards, 73, born in Maryland [BG-0712]. As Dr Safety S. Richards, born December 1851, he practised medicine in Jersey Township in 1900 with his wife Mary E. and sons Perry H. and Clarence [BG-0723], and in Newark in 1910 and 1920, still a physician at 68 [BG-0728; BG-0729].
