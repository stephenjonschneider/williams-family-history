What the records say

He married Catharine Williams on 10 Nov 1821 (L-0416). The partition of her father Daniel Williams's (G8) land in 1832–33 allotted Aaron Layton and Catharine his wife Lot 2, 17.50 a. (L-0201), which they sold to her brother Daniel (G7) on 30 July 1833 (L-0210). In 1841 Thomas W. Layton's lot in Howell adjoined Aaron Layton (L-0434). The 1865 census shows Aaron and Catherine Layton near Gilbert and Elizabeth (G7) Layton in Howell (L-0418). The Howell return of deaths for 1873 records Aaron Layton, married, 80, farmer, parents Tunis Layton, dying in February 1873, and his wife Catherine in April (L-0402); administration was granted on 17 Feb 1873 to William S. Clayton of Ocean County (L-0281). The death return's age of 80 puts his birth c. 1793, against the tree's 29 Dec 1794.
