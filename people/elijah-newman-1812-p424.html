What the records say

Elijah Newman and his brother Nelson signed the December 1847 notice asking the Orphans Court to divide the farm of their late father, Joseph I. Newman (G8), on the Cross Branch of Wreck Pond (N-0044). The court's 1848 division gave Elijah one-sixth as an heir, alongside Silas (G7), Nelson, Rhoda Allen, Lummus W. and Deborah Brand's children (N-0016, N-0014). The 1840 census lists his household with several boys (N-0053), and the 1850 census shows him as a farmer of 38 with his wife Sarah and children Joseph, Bloomfield, Britton, Elisha, Charles, Henry and Sarah (N-0220). A 1962 genealogy query records that an Elijah Newman married Sarah Rogers in 1830 (N-0229).
