What the records say

On 17 Mar 1847 a Monmouth justice of the peace married Samuel Bennet and Mary Layton; the return was recorded on 6 Nov 1847 (S-0143). Mary Bennett's death certificate records that she died at Key East, Neptune Township, on 22 Dec 1889, aged 59, married, born in Wall Township and a resident all her life; it names her father "Safty Layton" and her mother "Abby" (S-0131). Abigail Alegor had married Safety Layton in 1829 (S-0093) and took administration of his estate in 1840 (S-0068). Her son Andrew Bennett, born at Neptune City in Aug 1871, named his parents as Sam Bennett and Mary (S-0142), and Samuel and Mary Bennett were the parents of Holmes C. Bennett of West Avon (S-0140).
