What the records say

Jannetje Jans, wife of Jan Brouwer, is the mother named at the New Amsterdam baptisms of their children, including Pieter on 20 October 1660 [BR-0246, BR-0247]. The Jan Brouwer DNA site gives her marriage as c.1653, probably at Amsterdam, and her death as September 1683 at Flatlands [BR-0247].
