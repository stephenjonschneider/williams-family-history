What the records say

John Layton's (G10) will of 1 Mar 1761 names Safety as a son, devisee and executor (L-0054). He and George Rhea gave Oliver DeLancey and Henry Cuyler Jr a bond at Freehold on 5 Nov 1762 and were sued on it in 1764 (L-0425). In Nov 1763 Anthony Dennis surveyed 16 a. for him on the south side of Long Brook (L-0474). He signed his brother William's (G9) marriage bond in Nov 1764 (L-0490), and he and Christian Fleming were licensed to marry on 4 Jan 1765 (L-0414, L-0455). On 20 May 1767 Safety Layton of Shrewsbury, yeoman, mortgaged 100 a. between the Mill Brook and Long Brook to Isaac Vandorn, beside his brothers' shares (L-0055, L-0223, L-0224). His will of 20 Mar 1790, proved 8 May 1790, leaves his son James 10s and the rest to his wife, his daughter Rebecah and 'my 3 youngest sons' Joseph, Thomas and Safety, Thomas and Safety to be put to trades, with his friend Benjamin Jackson as executor (L-0451). The tree's 1761 marriage date is wrong (T11).
