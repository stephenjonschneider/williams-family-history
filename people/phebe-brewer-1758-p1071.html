What the records say

Her father Aaron Brewer's will of 7 April 1800 leaves £50 to his daughter Phebe Lewis [W-0324]. In 1791 Aaron deeded 20 acres to Joseph Lewis, who that March had also received 100 acres from John Lewis; on 1 November 1815 "Joseph Lewis and Phebe his wife", then of Ontario County, New York, sold about 30 acres of that land in Howell to Peter Smith through their attorney John Cooper [W-0384]. A side entry of 1810 shows a John Lewis and Phebe his wife of Howell selling 59 acres between Long Brook and the Manasquan at Aaron Brewer's corners [W-0391]; which Lewis was Phebe Brewer's husband is still to be settled.
