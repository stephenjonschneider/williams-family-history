What the records say

In 1919 Edward Williams married Gertrude V. Lomell, the sister of Ruth Lomell (G4), who married his brother Harry (G4) five years later (W-0424, W-0404). His mother-in-law Pauline Lomell's 1948 death notice names his wife as Mrs Edward Williams (W-0427), and his father Thomas E. Williams's (G5) 1956 obituary names him among the surviving children (W-0414). Gertrude's obituary, about 1987–88, names him as her surviving husband, Edward A., and their children Barry L. of Port Salerno, Florida, and Ruth Pearce of Stafford. It says the couple lived most of their lives in Lakewood and Point Pleasant before moving to Manahawkin and then, about 1981, to Barnegat Township (W-0430).
