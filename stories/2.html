SALEM TO GRAVESEND · 1631–1665

The Bownes

William Bowne of Yorkshire was at Salem, Massachusetts, by the mid-1630s. The First Church's own register lists him and his wife Anne as members and records the baptisms of his sons: “James son of William Bound” in 1636 (James, 25 August), Andrew in 1637 and Peter in 1639. About 1645 the family moved to Gravesend with Lady Moody's company and took a planter's lot there. On 26 December 1665, James Bowne married Mary Stout at Gravesend, joining the Bowne and Stout families. William's other son, Captain John Bowne, was Abraham Lincoln's 4th great-grandfather.
