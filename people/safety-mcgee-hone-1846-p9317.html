What the records say

His obituary records that Safety McGee Hone was born in Hocking County, Ohio, on 16 August 1846 and died at his home in Logan on 23 December 1907. He married Sarah M. Heinlein in September 1872; their only child was a son, Russell B. Hone. He joined the Methodist Episcopal church at Ilesboro in 1871, lived for eleven years after his marriage on his mother's farm, then seventeen years at Mount Pleasant, and his last seven at Logan. His wife, son, two grandchildren, his mother, two brothers and six sisters survived him [BG-0725]. His gravestone, with that of Sadie M. Hone (1852-1918), gives 1846-1907 [BG-0720].
