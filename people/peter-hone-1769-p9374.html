What the records say

In 1781 the Cumberland County Orphans' Court divided the estate of Henry Hone between his widow Ruth, "Peter the Eldest son", and Catharine, Christian, Nancy, Ruth, Margaret, Polly and Henry [S-0286]. Peter and Ruth Hone headed households in Washington County, Pennsylvania, in 1790 [S-0287]. Peter bought 121 acres in Peter Township from William McCord in April 1795 and sold 81 acres of it with his wife Mary in June 1796 [S-0288, S-0289]. By 1802 he was a justice of the peace in Jefferson County, Ohio; he married Margaret Bigger in 1811 and sold out in 1814 [S-0290, S-0292, S-0293]; in 1815 he released his share of his mother Ruth's "Widow's Purchase" on Mingo Creek [S-0294]. In September 1837 he swore, as a witness, to the will of Ray Benefield before the Knox County, Indiana, probate clerk [S-0305].
