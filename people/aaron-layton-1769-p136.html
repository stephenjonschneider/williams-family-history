What the records say

Aaron Layton carried the chain on pine-land surveys between the branches of the Metedeconk in 1788–91 and had 30 a. surveyed for himself in Mar 1788 beside land of Tunis Layton (L-0307, L-0308, L-0316). His father William's (G9) will of 1793 names him as eldest son (L-0057). In 1794 James Hankinson sold him land on the North Branch (L-0066); in 1797 he took title to the South Branch Metedeconk mills (L-0128), selling half of them with his wife Sarah's consent in 1802 (L-0070). He bought and sold North Branch land from 1808 to 1818, giving 30 a. to his son Tunis Jr in 1808 (L-0082, L-0083, L-0089, L-0112, L-0130, L-0137, L-0138). He married Sarah Barber on 24 June 1814 (L-0416), and in 1816 released his share of his father's homestead to Aaron Layton Jr (L-0115). His will of 20 Jan 1820 left his land to his daughters, who sold their ninths in 1832 (L-0192, L-0199); Silas Newman (G7, Newman side) bought the North Branch farm whole in 1846 (L-0389, L-0390).
