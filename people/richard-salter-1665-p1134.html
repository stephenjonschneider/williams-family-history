What the records say

Richard Salter (often written Saltar) was a lawyer, judge and member of the colonial Assembly in Monmouth County. In this workbook he appears as the attorney who conveyed William Dockwra's land to Andrew Bowne in 1705/6 (BG-0331) and in a bond of 1705 (BG-0647). He married Sarah Bowne, daughter of Capt. John Bowne, and their daughter Hannah married Mordecai Lincoln Jr by 1714 (BG-0735). The ahnentafel's birth "1665, England" and death "1730" are not verified; he is generally given as living after 1728.
