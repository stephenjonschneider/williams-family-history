What the records say

Elizabeth Hance, called 'Elizabeth Hance junior' to tell her from her mother, married John Worthly at the Shrewsbury Friends' meeting house in March 1695/6 (WO-0187). Her father John Hance of Shrewsbury, one of the 1685 Manasquan purchasers, named her 'Elizabeth Worthly' among his children in his will of 24 March 1707/8 (WO-0189). The Shrewsbury register lists her eight children with John Worthly, born 1696 to 1712, including Richard (1712), the grandfather of Lydia Borden (G8) who married Amos Wolcott, and records her death on 7 May 1749 (WO-0188).
