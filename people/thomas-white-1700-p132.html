What the records say

Thomas White farmed at Deal in Shrewsbury, on land his grandfather the carpenter had held before 1685 [WO-0315, WO-0318]. The Friends register lists ten children born to him and his wife Elizabeth between 1725/6 and 1748/9, among them Mary, born 19 April 1730 [WO-0191]. Mary married Richard Worthley at his house in 1747 [WO-0194]. Stillwell's genealogy identifies Elizabeth as Elizabeth Borden, born 1707, who died in 1760 [WO-0318]. In 1762 Thomas married the widow Deborah Irons. His will of December 1775 divides the Deal plantation between his sons Thomas and Jacob and names his daughter Mary Worthley [WO-0192]. His inventory was taken in November 1778 [WO-0192]. In 1714 the dividers of his father's estate allotted him a quarter of the plantation at Deal [WO-0321]. In 1773 he and his sons Thomas and Jacob witnessed the will of his brother-in-law James Tucker, whose son John he named his 'nephew' and executor [WO-0323, WO-0192].
