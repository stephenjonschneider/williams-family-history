What the records say

His father Safety Meghee of Bordentown, cordwainer, left his estate on 10 January 1770 to his five children Amy, William, Mary, Safety and Sarah [BG-0697]. Safety McGee married Rachel Cadwallader at the Abington Presbyterian Church, Pennsylvania, on 30 December 1790 [BG-0702]. In old age he told the historian of the Hart family that he had known Col. Joseph Hart of Bucks County, seen the army camped on his plantation, attended him in his last illness and carried word of his death to his brother at Hopewell, New Jersey; he died, the book says, at nearly one hundred [BG-0713].
