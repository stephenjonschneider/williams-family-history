What the records say

His gravestone at Adelphia gives his birth as 11 February 1802 and his death as 26 May 1843 (W-0316). On 18 March 1826 a justice married him to Elizabeth Layton (G7) "of Freehold" (W-0228), and the 1830 and 1840 censuses show their growing Howell household (W-0232). In 1830 he bought 30.25 acres on the North Branch of the Metedeconk from Andrew Bell (W-0226). When the heirs of his father, Daniel Williams (G8), divided the Miery Branch homestead in 1832–33, Daniel received two of the four lots, beside his sister Catharine Layton and the heirs of his sister Mary Skidmore (W-0225); Aaron and Catharine Layton sold him their lot in 1833 (W-0227). A coroner's inquest records his death by suicide on 26 May 1843 (W-0315). His widow and Israel Reynolds administered the estate (W-0229), and in 1849 the Orphans' Court named his nine children as heirs, among them Daniel D. (G6) (W-0230).
