What the records say

Donato DiCorcia is known so far only from the compiler's family tree, which gives him as born in 1810 and as the father, with his wife Catarina DiLuca, of Francesco 'Frank' DiCorcia, the stone mason who settled in Jersey City [W-0554]. His grandson Donato 'Harry' DiCorcia, born in 1882, carried his name. No Italian civil record, passenger list or other document yet names him, and the family's town in Italy is unknown. Frank's naturalization papers (1903) or Harry D.'s 1950 death certificate are the likeliest papers to lead to him.
