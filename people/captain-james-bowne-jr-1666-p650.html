What the records say

Administration of James Bowne's (G12?) estate was granted to his son James on 14 Mar 1695/6, with Capt. Andrew Bowne as fellow bondsman (BG-0019, BG-0294). In 1697 the Proprietors patented land to him "in right of his father James Bowne late of Middletown deceased" (BG-0399), and he witnessed family deeds of 1695–1708 (BG-0169, BG-0398). As heir at law to his brother William, he quitclaimed the Crosswicks land of William's wife Rebecca to her brother James Grover on 30 Dec 1720 (BG-0200). His brother John the cordwainer held Crosswicks land "released to me by my brother James Bowne" (BG-0340). Reading says he had two sons, James (d. 1750) and Obadiah (d. 1761) (BG-0518).
