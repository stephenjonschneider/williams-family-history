What the records say

Henry Williams, the youngest of Daniel and Elizabeth Williams's nine children, kept the family close to the old homestead. In 1878 he bought from his wife's family the northern part of the 1788 tract his great-grandfather had settled [W-0317], kept a store at Southard and later Asbury Park, and in 1894 let his nephew Thomas E. Williams farm it [W-0512]. A Bethel Methodist for over fifty years, he died near Southard in March 1917 and was buried in the family plot at Bethel [W-0509]. His will left the homestead farm to his son Charles T. and the Reynolds farm to W.H.H. Jr, who passed it to his sister Edna Sharp in 1918 [W-0491, W-0511]; the woodland was auctioned in 1917 [W-0510].
