What the records say

Richard Stout (G13?) is listed in Gravesend's first division about 1643 (BG-0521) and among the Monmouth patentees of 1665 (BG-0133); he took the Middletown overseers' oath in 1669, signing by mark (BG-0171). His 1675 headright counted "himselfe his wife foure sonns & three daughters" (BG-0426). He bought his homestead, Stouts Place, from the Ramanessin sachems in 1675 and patented it in 1676 (BG-0425, BG-0431), with a second patent in 1677 (BG-0434). He and his wife Penelope (G13?) sold land in 1679/80 and acknowledged the sale in court in 1684 (BG-0006, BG-0122). From 1688 he gave land to his sons (BG-0443). His will of 9 June 1703 names his wife, sons John, Richard, James, Jonathan, David and Benjamin, daughters Mary (G12?), Alice and Sarah, and Peter's family (BG-0310); he was dead by 5 Oct 1705 (BG-0231).
