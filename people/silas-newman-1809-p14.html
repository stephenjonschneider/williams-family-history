What the records say

John Saplin Newman, an elder of the Methodist Independent Church, married Silas to Elizabeth Cooper (G7) on 27 November 1831 (N-0301) and bought land in Howell in 1832 (N-0001). In 1842 he moved to Harmony, now Jackson (N-0002), and in 1844 he and Elizabeth gave the Harmony Methodist church its lot (N-0003). In 1846 he bought North Branch farms (N-0004, N-0005). After his father Joseph I. Newman (G8) died, letters of administration went to Lydia (G8) and Silas (N-0015); in 1847 he sold his full sixth as heir at law; a grandson's share fetched one-seventh the price (N-0008, N-0290). In 1850 he was a farmer of 40 with seven children, Catherine Louisa (G6) among them (N-0220). An official member of the Freehold Methodist circuit by 1837 (N-0300), he officiated at a Howell wedding as 'Rev. Silas Newman' on 21 May 1859 (N-0294). He died on 10 August 1859, in the 24th year of his ministry; friends raised a marble monument at Harmony (N-0302). His daughter's 1862 marriage return names him as her father (N-0255).
