Strong — Safety Grover (G12?, P02); mother not identified: Daniel Herbert's will (1747) calls Safety Grover the "honoured grandfather" of his wife Amy, whose father was Safety Borden (his consent to her marriage, 1745); Safety Borden's own grandfather was Benjamin Borden, so the Grover grandfather came through Amy's mother; Stillwell (no. 21) gives the same marriage (BG-0666, BG-0560, BG-0531).

What the records say

This daughter of Capt. Safety Grover (G12?) is known only through her family. She married her first cousin Safety Borden of Crosswicks, son of Benjamin Borden and Abigail Grover (Stillwell no. 21, BG-0531, BG-0738). Their daughter Amy married William Maghee of Allentown, then Daniel Herbert in 1745 with her father Safety Borden's consent; Herbert's will of 1747 names Amy's "honoured grandfather, Safety Grover" - which, since Amy's Borden grandfather was Benjamin, places a Grover in her mother's line (BG-0666). Safety Borden's will of 1757 names his grandsons Safety and James Meghee (BG-0560). Her own first name has not been found; whether she is the "Martha" of Safety Borden's 1757 will is open (Q48).
