What the records say

Phebe was the widow and an executrix of Thomas Cooper (G10?): on 17 May 1755 "Phebe Cooper, Thomas Cooper & John Williams, executors of Thomas Cooper deceased" sued Albert and William Covenhoven of Freehold for a £200 debt (C-0022). On 31 May 1758 a licence was issued for Phebe Cooper of Freehold to marry Benjamin Woolley of Shrewsbury, very possibly this widow (C-0396). The compiler's tree makes her the mother of Ellenor Neeltje (Cooper) Brewer (G9) and gives her 1708–1785; these dates are not yet supported by a record (C-0031). Ellenor named a daughter Phebe.
