What the records say

Henry H. Wolcott was born at Eatontown on 25 August 1802 and died on 26 February 1874, according to his wife Harriet's obituary, which places his family among the old Shrewsbury Quakers (WO-0111). The 1850 census shows him at Millstone as a farmer with land worth $4,000, with Harriet and their children Andrew, Charles, Amos (G6), Asher and Anna (WO-0083). The 1851 county map marks his house and store at the Burnt Tavern crossroads (WO-0067). Court papers show a carpenter working on his house in 1845, a fine in 1847 for selling apple spirits by the glass without a licence, and a debt case in 1858 (WO-0064, WO-0065, WO-0070). He witnessed a survey in the Metedeconk woods in 1845 (WO-0095). His will names his wife, his sons Charles H., Amos A. and Asher M., and his daughters Mary E. Vaughn, Lydia A. Garret and Anna M. Conover (WO-0060); his daughters' 1876 receipts for their legacies survive (WO-0142). A county history and family compilations name his parents as Amos and Lydia (WO-0075, WO-0081).
