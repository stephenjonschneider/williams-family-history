What the records say

James Grover Junr held a headright "in right of himselfe & his wife" in 1676 and had his house lot and Poplar Field land surveyed in 1677 (BG-0409, BG-0381). With his brothers-in-law he executed his father's will of 1685 (BG-0286, BG-0287). As a carpenter he sold a Poplar Field lot to John Stout in 1697 (BG-0423). As wheelwright and "son and heir to James Grover deceased" he partitioned the mill estate with William Winter in 1708 (BG-0188, BG-0189), sold the homestead to James Patterson in 1711 (BG-0191), and in 1714 sold Winter the old mill's stones and iron work (BG-0527). His will of 18 Mar 1714/15, proved 16 Apr 1715, names his wife Alice and daughters Rebecka Gibbons, Hester Giberson and Martha Vane, and a grandson James Patterson (BG-0524, BG-0527).
