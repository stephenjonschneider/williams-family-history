What the records say

Benjamin Borden witnessed James Grover's gift to Safety Grover in Nov 1685 (S-0076), and James Grover's will of 1 Dec 1685 names him as son-in-law, husband of Abigail (S-0098). In 1696 he bought land in Freehold on the Burlington Path (S-0079). He was among the rioters at the Middletown court in 1701 (S-0103), and by Mar 1715 had "removed out of the County" (S-0107). In Sep 1716, as Benjamin Borden of Ancocas, Burlington County, he deeded land to "my son James" (S-0085). Stillwell's Borden account lists his children (S-0108).
