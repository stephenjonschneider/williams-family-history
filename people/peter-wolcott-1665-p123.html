What the records say

Peter Woolcott appears in the Burlington Friends men's minutes from 1685: the meeting paid him for work on the burying ground and the meeting house, and in early 1686 appointed Friends to care for him and his wife, being poor (WO-0096, WO-0097, WO-0098, WO-0101, WO-0102). He witnessed a Rancocas Creek deed in 1686 (WO-0009). The Friends birth register records his son Benjamin, born 20 May 1687 to Peter and Jone (WO-0089), and the death register records his own death on 4 August 1690 (WO-0090). His widow Joane remarried in 1695 (WO-0138). Family trees and a family society make him a son of Samuel Wolcott of Shrewsbury (WO-0004, WO-0075); a 1912 genealogy says Nathaniel instead (WO-0108).
