What the records say

Jeremiah Newman Jr kept the family Bible that records his birth on 10 June 1780, his marriage to Phebe Newman on 9 April 1800, his parents John and Rebecca, and his wife's parents Peter (G9) and Phebe (G9) (N-0199). From 1804 he bought land on the main North Branch and the Horse Pond Branch of Wreck Pond (N-0162, N-0171, N-0191). In 1828 he and Zebedee Newman were granted administration of Peter Newman's estate, and in 1829 he and Phebe signed the release of Peter's land (N-0020, N-0013). In 1836 he petitioned, as a son of John Newman, for the division of his father's land (N-0093, N-0107). The 1850 census shows him at 70, a farmer, with Phebe, 68 (N-0220). His Bible gives his death as 6 November 1859.
