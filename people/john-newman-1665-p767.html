What the records say

On 28 March 1691 John Newman of Shrewsbury, carpenter, and his wife Mary sold land near the North River to William Scott (N-0058, N-0115). A 1688 ejectment record names him as a landholder by the highway from the Shrewsbury Iron Works (N-0112). Surveys and patents for Joseph West in 1690–1702 name John Newman as an adjoining owner on the Falls River, holding land formerly George Curless's (N-0057, N-0114, N-0148, N-0149).
