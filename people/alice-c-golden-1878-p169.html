What the records say

On Sunday 25 April 1909 Miss Alice C. Golden, daughter of Mr. and Mrs. Joseph A. Golden of Scobeyville, married Archibald Newman at the Methodist parsonage; the couple were to live for a time with her parents (N-0282). The 1910 census shows Alice, 32, as Archibald's wife on Farmingdale Road in South Shrewsbury, the mother of one child, then living, with their daughter Sadie in the house (N-0287). By June 1915 Archibald was a widower, and a son, George G., had been born in February 1914 (N-0288). The family tree gives her death at New Bedford on 16 February 1914 (N-0286).
