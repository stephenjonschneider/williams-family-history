What the records say

Safety Borden witnessed and proved his father Benjamin's gift of land to his brother James in Sept 1716 (S-0085). In Feb 1718 James made "my Trusty & Loving Brother Safety Borden" his attorney (S-0087), and Safety witnessed another deed that month (S-0088). He sold land near Crosswicks to Abraham Lincon, recited in 1722 (S-0012), was named a highway surveyor there in 1733 (S-0016) and is a neighbour in a deed of 1734 (S-0017). A 1777 mortgage recites land he bought from a John Bowne (S-0090). His will, made at Bordentown on 18 Nov 1757 and proved 10 Sept 1761, names his wife Martha and her children Richard Walton and Martha Stevenson, his son Richard, a daughter Hannah, his brother Joseph, his granddaughter Cathrine Britten and his grandsons Safty and James Meghee (S-0135). Stillwell gives his birth on 6 Sep 1682, a death in Nov 1757 that is really the will's date, and a first wife "supposed" to be a daughter of Safety Grover (S-0108), whose daughter Amy married William Maghee (S-0112).
