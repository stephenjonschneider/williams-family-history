What the records say

Thomas White, carpenter, bought land at Shrewsbury from Thomas Potter and Judah Allen in 1675; tradition says he came from Deal in Kent and named the place [WO-0318]. He died without a will by January 1684/5, when Thomas Potter, Samuel Dennis and Symon Charles valued his estate at £140; his son Samuel administered and paid debts to, among others, John Worthley and Thomas White junior [WO-0315]. In 1688 Samuel was patented 617 acres by the sea at Long Pond in his father's right [WO-0318]. The Proprietors had patented him 560 acres at Deal in 1681, and in 1688 they confirmed the tract to Samuel 'in right of his father Thomas White deceased' [WO-0322].
