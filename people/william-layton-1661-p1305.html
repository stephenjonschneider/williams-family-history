What the records say

On 22 Aug 1690 Richard Hartshorne sold land on Pesaquanogus Brook to 'William Laton, Juner', the price paid by William Layton of Middletown (L-0023). His father's (G12) will of 1702 names him among the sons (L-0022, L-0413). On 10 Aug 1704 William Layton of Freehold, planter, sold part of that land to his brother Samuel (L-0025), and later Freehold deeds use 'William Layton's corner, formerly John Scott's' as a bound (L-0059, L-0037, L-0038). His will of 10 Apr 1731, as William Layton of Freehold, yeoman, leaves his wife Mary land and negroes for life and gives the Scott land to his 'cousin William Layton Junior', son of his brother Samuel; it was proved 15 May 1735 (L-0047).
