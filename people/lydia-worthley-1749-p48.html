What the records say

Lydia Worthley, born in October 1749, was the daughter of Richard Worthley of Shrewsbury and his second wife Mary, daughter of Thomas and Elizabeth (Borden) White (WO-0180). She was licensed to marry John Borden on 28 January 1767 (WO-0178). As Lydia Borden, wife of John, she was a member of Shrewsbury Monthly Meeting and died in the first month of 1831; the NYGBR gives the day as 16 January 1831 (WO-0181, WO-0180). A family tree names John Borden and Lydia Worthley as the parents of Lydia Borden (G8), wife of Amos Wolcott (WO-0003). She was not the daughter of the John Worthly who married Lydia Bowne in 1747 — that couple's recorded child was Ann (WO-0180).
