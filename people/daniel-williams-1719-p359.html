What the records say

Daniel Williams of Upper Freehold sued John Cox on bonds of 1761 and 1763 (W-0362, W-0363), and in 1768 the Cox family mortgaged land "bought of Daniel Williams" (W-0155). In 1786 Daniel and his wife Hannah sold half of his share in the Manahawkin tracts to John Lloyd (W-0182). His will of 10 May 1789, proved 30 June 1789, left the lot on the south side of Metedeconk Bridge to David Lewis and Jane Estill and returned the Stafford lands to the daughters of his brother, John Williams of Freehold; it names no children of his own (W-0063). His widow Hannah and his executors John Lloyd and John Richmond were sued for his debts, and the sheriff sold the Manahawkin land in 1799 (W-0252).
