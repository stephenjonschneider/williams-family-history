What the records say

Nelson Newman signed, with his brother Elijah, the December 1847 notice for the division of the farm of their late father, Joseph I. Newman (G8), on the Cross Branch of Wreck Pond (N-0044). The Orphans Court division of 1848 gave him one-sixth as an heir, in common with Silas (G7) and the other children (N-0016, N-0014). The 1840 census lists him as the head of a young household (N-0053). In 1841 he bought 20.04 acres from his father (N-0009). He died at Wall on 15 January 1884, and his estate was administered by Jeremiah F. Newman and William L. Tilton (N-0022).
