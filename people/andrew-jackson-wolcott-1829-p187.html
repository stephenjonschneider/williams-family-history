What the records say

The 1850 census lists Andrew Jackson Wolcott, 21, a laborer, in the Millstone household of Henry H. (G7) and Harriet Wolcott (G7) (WO-0083). His father's will leaves a legacy to 'Henrietta, wife of Andrew J. Wolcott' (WO-0060), and a county biography names him among Henry H.'s children (WO-0081).
