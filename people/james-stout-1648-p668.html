What the records say

James Stout of Freehold, yeoman, bought land from George Willocks in 1705 (BG-0302). On 29 Jan 1715 he and his wife Elizabeth, signing by mark, sold to John Warford and to Thomas Williams the land "whereon the said James Stout now lives", keeping a half-acre burying ground (BG-0033, BG-0034). He was one of the bond debtors of John Bowne the merchant in 1716 (BG-0296). Deeds of 1717 and 1721 describe the tract as the James Stout land, he being the "late" owner (BG-0206, BG-0043). Stillwell identifies him as Richard Stout's son James (BG-0302).
