Also known as
Helena Van Cleve; Hilletie Van Cleeff; Hilletje Van Kleef

Probable — Isbrant Van Cleef (G11, P59) & Jannetje Aertse Van der Bilt (G11, P60): "Isbrant Van Cleve and Janneke his wife" sponsored her child on 11 Apr 1726, the usual place for the mother's parents. Isbrant had his own child baptized at the same church two weeks before her first child (1724). Her name Hilletje matches Jannetje's mother, Hilletje Remsen, and her son Aris matches Jannetje's father, Aris Van der Bilt (BR-0249, BR-0245). Bergen (1881) instead lists "Helena, m. John Brower of N. J." under Benjamin Van Cleef (BR-0245); that is set aside, since the church sponsorship points to Isbrant. No estate paper names her (BR-0243).

What the records say

As the wife of Jan Brouwer she appears in the Freehold and Middletown Dutch registers as "(blank) Van Kleve" in June 1724, "Helena Van Cleve" in April 1726, "Hilletie" in 1727 and "Hilletje Van Kleef" in 1735 and 1738 [BR-0249]. Her 1726 child was sponsored by Isbrant Van Cleve and Janneke his wife, and her son Benjamin in 1738 by Benjamin Van Kleef and Rachel Couwenhove [BR-0249]. On 28 November 1731 she and Jan sponsored Jan, son of Hans Brouwer [BR-0248]. Bergen's 1881 register places a Helena who "m. John Brower of N. J." among Benjamin Van Cleef's children [BR-0245]. Isbrant Van Cleef's 1728/9 inventory names no heirs [BR-0243].
