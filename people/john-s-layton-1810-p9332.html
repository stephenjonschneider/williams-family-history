What the records say

In June 1855 "J. S. Laton", 45, a New Jersey-born laborer, his wife E., 47, and son S., 14, had lived one year in Brooklyn's 14th Ward [S-0218]. In June 1860 John Layton, 50, a New Jersey-born carpenter, lived in Brooklyn's 14th Ward (Williamsburgh) with his wife Eliza M., 50, sons Garrett (27, carpenter), John H. (21, carpenter) and Safety (19, apprentice carpenter), and Hannah Sporten (24) with her son William (3), all born in New Jersey [S-0204]. In May 1863 John H. and Safety were enrolled for the draft at one address with Nathan J. Layton [S-0201], and in 1865 John, 55, Eliza, 55 (mother of six), John H. and Safety with his wife Emma shared a brick house in the 14th Ward [S-0198]. Safety's muster card gives his birthplace as Farmingdale, New Jersey [S-0205]. John's middle initial is written T., S. or L.
