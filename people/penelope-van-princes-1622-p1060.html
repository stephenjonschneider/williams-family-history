What the records say

Penelope (G13?) joined her husband Richard Stout (G13?) in selling land on 26 Feb 1679/80 and came into court in person to acknowledge it on 23 Sept 1684 (BG-0006, BG-0122). The Middletown Town Book, as quoted by Stillwell, names her daughter "Mary, daughter of Richard and Penelope Stout" (BG-0299). Richard's will of 9 June 1703 provides for "my loving wife" with a life estate in the orchard and homestead (BG-0310). The story of her shipwreck, her wounding by Indians and her rescue, her Van Princis maiden name and her death at 110 in 1732 come from family tradition printed in 1907 and later (BG-0510); no record held confirms them.
