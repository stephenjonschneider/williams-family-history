What the records say

James Grover (G13?) appears in Gravesend's first division about 1643 and with a 20-acre farm there in 1646 (BG-0521, BG-0268). He was one of the Monmouth patentees of 1665 (BG-0133), Middletown's first town clerk and a deputy to the 1668 Assembly (BG-0134, BG-0535). Patents of 1676 and 1677 granted him Grovers Inheritance on Swimming River and Grovers New Invention at his mill on Mill Creek (BG-0408, BG-0383), and he sat as a justice in 1677 (BG-0181). In Nov–Dec 1685 he gave land to his sons Safety and Joseph and made his will naming his wife Rebecca and children James, Joseph, Safety, Abigail (Borden) and Hannah (Gardiner) (BG-0010, BG-0412, BG-0286). His funeral was charged on 28 Dec 1685 (BG-0287). Compiled sources suggest a christening at Great Berkhamsted in 1621; that is a candidate only (BG-0266).
