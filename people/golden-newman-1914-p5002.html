What the records say

The 1915 state census lists George G. Newman, born February 1914, in Armenia Newman's Wall household with her widowed son Archibald and Saddie (N-0288). In 1920 Golden, 6, is Armenia's grandson in the same household (N-0097), and in 1930 Golden, 17, is Archibald's son, a painter like his father (N-0284). The family tree lists him as Golden, born about 1913 (N-0286).
