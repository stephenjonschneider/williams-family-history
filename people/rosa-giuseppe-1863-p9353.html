What the records say

Rosa Giuseppe is known so far only from the compiler's family tree, which gives her as born in 1863 and as the mother, with her husband Joseph, of Maria Gerarda Giuseppe, the Mary J. DiCorcia who married Harry DiCorcia and died at Point Pleasant in 1945 [W-0554]. Her maiden name is not known — the tree gives her only as Rosa Giuseppe. No census, passenger list or church record yet names her, and it is not known where in Italy the family lived or whether she came to America. Mary's 1945 death certificate and her marriage record are the likeliest papers to name her.
