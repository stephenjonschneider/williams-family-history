What the records say

Christian Fleming of Monmouth married Safety Layton by licence of 4 Jan 1765, with George Harrison as surety (S-0091). She is probably the unnamed wife in his will of 20 Mar 1790, who was to share his estate with their daughter Rebecah and their three youngest sons (S-0048). Stillwell's Layton article gives her name as Christiana (S-0074). The Ancestry tree's marriage date of 19 Dec 1761 belongs to another couple.
