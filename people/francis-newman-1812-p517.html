What the records say

Francis Newman is named among the fifteen children and heirs of John Newman in the 1836 Orphans Court petition, and his objection, through William L. Dayton, stayed and then ended that division (N-0107, N-0108, N-0109). In 1844–49 his brothers and sisters released their eighths of their father's farm to him (N-0232, N-0237). The 1850 census lists him as a farmer of 38 in Howell with Mary, 34, and children Huldah, John, Helena, Catharine, William, George, Mariette and Ruth (N-0094). In 1870 he farmed in Wall, worth $4,000, with Mary A., William, George W. and Hampton (N-0095). His son George W.'s 1872 marriage return names him as the father (N-0279). The family tree gives his marriage to Mary Ann Bearmore on 9 November 1831 and burial in the Bearmore ground at West Belmar (N-0101, N-0286).
