What the records say

The Friends' register records Peter's birth on 28 September 1753 (WO-0092). As a young man he was an apprentice to Ralph Allen at Chesterfield (WO-0100). He married Lydia Potter at the Shrewsbury meeting house on 26 June 1777, with his mother 'Cleffey' and his brothers as witnesses, and their children are entered in the Friends' register (WO-0049, WO-0051, WO-0144). He sued Captain William Schenck for a large debt in 1788 (WO-0062, WO-0063). As executor of his father Benjamin (G9) he sold land in 1792–1800 (WO-0020, WO-0027, WO-0139–WO-0141). He bought lots in 1791 and 1808, gave land for a Friends' schoolhouse in 1802, and in 1801 mortgaged his mill pond with its dam and flood gates (WO-0025, WO-0033, WO-0028, WO-0044); a 1806 deed calls him a miller (WO-0029). He died without a will; his inventory lists mill stones, and his land was divided in 1820 among his widow and children (WO-0055, WO-0116, WO-0118). He was a brother of Amos (G8).
