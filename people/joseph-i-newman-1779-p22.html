What the records say

In January 1801 Robert Montgomery sold a 74-acre tract on the Cross Branch of Rack (Wreck) Pond to 'Joseph Newman, son of John Newman Junr' (N-0155). Joseph added neighbouring lots in 1804 (N-0011, N-0161), 1811 (N-0170) and 1814 (N-0177), and in 1830 he bought from Montgomery's executors as Joseph I. Newman (N-0012). On 8 April 1829 Joseph I. Newman and his wife Lydia (G8) signed the release of Peter Newman's land among Peter's heirs (N-0013), and in 1833 a share of that land was described as set off 'for Joseph I. Newman and wife' (N-0035). He died without a will on 16 April 1846, and letters of administration went to Lydia and his son Silas (G7) in September 1847 (N-0015). In 1848 the Orphans Court divided his farm of about 200 acres near the Cross Branch into sixths for Silas, Elijah, Nelson, Rhoda Allen, Lummus W. and the children of Deborah Brand (N-0044, N-0014). A family burial ground on the farm was reserved in an 1848 deed; its graves have not been found (N-0018).
