What the records say

Richard George Lomell was baptized in Brooklyn on 26 January 1903; the register gives his birth at Boston on 20 March 1898 to George and Paulina Lomell (W-0429). He is in his parents' household in the 1900 census at Falmouth, in 1910 and 1920 in Brooklyn — by 1920 a druggist — and in 1930 on Trenton Avenue, Point Pleasant, where he was a single chemist at a steel works (W-0431, W-0425, W-0434, W-0426). His mother's 1948 death notice names him as Richard G. Lomell (W-0427), and the New Jersey death index records his death on 6 August 1968 (W-0433). The 1920 census gives his birthplace as New York; the baptism's Boston is preferred.
