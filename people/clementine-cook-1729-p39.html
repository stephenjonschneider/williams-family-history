What the records say

'Cleffey' Cook of Shrewsbury was licensed to marry Benjamin Wolcott (G9), a weaver of Shrewsbury, on 27 February 1753 (WO-0126). Benjamin's will of 31 July 1784 calls her 'my wife Clemey', gives her the use of the house and lot where she lived 'in order to support her Father during his life', and leaves the weave shop to 'all my Children and their mother' — Peter, Jacob, Benjamin, Elizabeth, Henry, Merebah and Amos (G8) (WO-0160). As 'Cleffey Wolcott' she signed her son Peter's Quaker marriage certificate in 1777 (WO-0049), and she signed among the family at her daughter Meribah's wedding in 1792 (WO-0050). The Shrewsbury tax lists charge 'Clemence Wolcott' on the home farm from 1794 to 1796 but not in 1797, so she died about then (WO-0114).
