What the records say

Mary White was born on the nineteenth of the second month (April) 1730, a daughter of Thomas and Elizabeth White of Shrewsbury; the meeting's register lists her with her brothers Samuel, Thomas and Jacob and her sisters (WO-0191). She married Richard Worthley at an appointed meeting held at her father Thomas White's house on 17 June 1747 (WO-0194). Her father's will of 1775 names her 'daughter Mary Worthley', and her sister Zilpha married a Woolley, which explains Richard's brother-in-law Nathan Woolley (WO-0192, WO-0186). Her daughter Lydia, born October 1749, married John Borden in 1767 (WO-0180, WO-0178). The NYGBR names her mother as Elizabeth Borden and gives her death as 25 May 1802 (WO-0180).
