What the records say

Abraham Lincoln, eldest son of John Lincoln and Rebecca Flowers, was born in Berks County, Pennsylvania, on 13 May 1744 and went with his parents to Linville Creek in Virginia. He married Bathsheba about 1770, served as a militia captain during the Revolution, and in the early 1780s took his family to Kentucky. In May 1786 he was shot while working in his field near Long Run, in sight of his young son Thomas - a story the President later told (BG-0735).
