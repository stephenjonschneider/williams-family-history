What the records say

John Borden of Shrewsbury, yeoman, made his will on 28 April 1768. He left his wife Elizabeth goods, cows and firewood; his eldest son John fifteen acres off the northwest corner of his plantation by Josiah Parker's land; his son Joel the rest of the land and the grist mill; and legacies to his daughters Mary (wife of Samuel Pintard), Elizabeth (wife of John Corlies), Anna, Jane and Hannah. Benjamin Wolcott (G9) was one of the three witnesses. The will was proved on 9 May 1772 (WO-0179). The NYGBR names John and Elizabeth Borden as the parents of John Borden (b. 1742), husband of Lydia Worthley (WO-0180).
