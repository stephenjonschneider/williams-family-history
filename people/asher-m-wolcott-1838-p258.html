What the records say

The 1850 census lists Asher M. Wolcott, 12, at school in his father Henry H. Wolcott's (G7) household at Millstone (WO-0083). His father's will names him as a son (WO-0060). A county biography of his brother Charles says Asher helped run the Riverside House at Toms River with his mother from 1863 to 1873 and was later a hotel man and farmer at Charleston Springs (WO-0081). His mother's obituary mentions him (WO-0111).
