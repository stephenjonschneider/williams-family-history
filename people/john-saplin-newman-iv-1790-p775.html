What the records say

John Saplin Newman was granted administration of the estate of his father, John Newman, on 6 April 1830 (N-0106, N-0110), and he is named among the fifteen children and heirs in the 1836 Orphans Court petition (N-0093, N-0107). In 1825 his brother Aaron sold him 42½ acres on the North Branch of Wreck Pond (N-0046). In 1845 he had land surveyed in Howell (N-0141), and in 1849 he bought a lot between the lands of Peter Newman and John Newman, deceased (N-0236). His will of 1848 was contested by seven of his children (N-0209). His North Branch land was sold by the sheriff in 1859 (N-0046). As an elder of the Methodist Independent Church he married his half-brother Francis Newman and Silas Newman (G7) in November 1831 (N-0301). He died of paralysis on 1 June 1853, aged 63, after nearly forty years as an unpaid minister of the gospel (N-0305).
