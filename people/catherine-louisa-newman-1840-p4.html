What the records say

The 1850 census lists Catharine, aged 9 and at school, in the Howell household of her father Silas Newman (G7) and mother Elizabeth (G7) (N-0220). On 7 September 1862 'C. L. Newman', 20, married Daniel D. Williams (G6) at Farmingdale; the return names her father, Silas Newman (N-0255). She appears as Louisa in the 1865 state census with Daniel and their son Thomas E. (G5), next door to her widowed mother (N-0273); as 'Lucy' in 1870 (N-0274); and as Louisa in 1880, with her mother in the household (N-0275). In 1900 she reported four children born, three living, although five children were in the house in 1880 (N-0276). Her death certificate records Katherine L. Williams, born 26 November 1840, died 2 March 1920, daughter of Silas Newman and Elizabeth Cooper, buried at Southard (N-0256). Her obituary says she died at her home in Lakewood, aged 79, survived by Thomas E., Mrs J. H. Clayton and Mrs William A. Cooper (N-0277). Daniel's 1937 death certificate names his wife Catherine L. Newman (N-0278).
