What the records say

The 1850 census lists Amos, 14, at school in his father Henry H. Wolcott's (G7) household at Millstone (WO-0083). A family Bible records his marriage to Annie Strickland on 22 June 1865 (WO-0078), five months before his daughter Mary Ann (G5) was born to Elizabeth Ann Clevenger (G6); Mary Ann's death certificate names him as her father (WO-0001). In 1870 he was a watch repairer at Keyport with land worth $6,000, sharing a house with his brother-in-law William Matthews (WO-0084); in 1880 he was a jeweler on Broad Street with Annie and their children Augusta and Telfair (WO-0085). Court papers show the firm of Wolcott & Matthews at Keyport in 1877–78 (WO-0073, WO-0074). His father's will made him a co-executor (WO-0060, WO-0142). In 1900 he was a watchmaker in Gainesville, Florida (WO-0086). He died at his daughter's home in New York on 7 April 1908; his obituaries give his birth at Charleston Springs on 16 January 1836 (WO-0076, WO-0077).
