What the records say

Phebe signed with her mark when she and her husband Peter Newman (G9) sold 10.44 acres south of Shark River to a Joseph Newman on 21 July 1800 (N-0010). The Bible kept by her son-in-law Jeremiah Newman Jr calls her 'my wife's mother' and records her death on 30 November 1820, aged 77 (N-0199). The same entry gives her birth as 12 June 1763, which cannot be right for the mother of a daughter born in 1780; her age at death points to about 1743. That daughter, Phebe, married Jeremiah Jr in 1800 and later willed her share of 'the lands of which my father Peter Newman died seized' (N-0199, N-0200). The Phebe who signed Peter's heirs' release in 1829 was the daughter (N-0013).
