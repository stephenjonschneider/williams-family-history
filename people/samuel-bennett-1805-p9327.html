What the records say

A Monmouth justice of the peace married Samuel Bennet and Mary Layton on 17 Mar 1847, the return being recorded on 6 Nov 1847 (S-0143). The 1880 census of Neptune Township lists Samuel Bennett, 75, farmer, with his wife Mary, 40, his son Andrew, 16, his daughter Lottie, 6, and Sarah Clevenger, 11; next door was Holmes G. Bennett, 32, carpet weaver (S-0144). Holmes C. Bennett, carpet weaver of West Avon, was named as son of Samuel and Mary Bennett on his death report (S-0140), and Sam Bennett and Mary are named as Andrew's parents on his 1923 death certificate (S-0142).
