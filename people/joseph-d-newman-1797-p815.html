What the records say

Joseph Numan of Shrewsbury, yeoman, signed by mark when he sold about 15 acres on the Squan River to Samuel Foster in 1791 (N-0153). In 1803 Joseph Newman 'of Squancom' sold land on the north and west side of 'the brook that runs through my father John['s land]' to Apollo Brinley (N-0159). By 1823 he was married to Faith, formerly Faith Herbert, and sued over the estate of Henry Herbert (N-0219, N-0216). Joseph and Faithful Newman sold small lots near Howell Furnace in 1823–29 (N-0246, N-0247, N-0251).
