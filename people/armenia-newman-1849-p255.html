What the records say

In 1870 Armenia, 21, lived in Wall with Elizabeth Woolley, 46, next to Francis Newman's family (N-0095). On 25 February 1872 Armenia Wooly, 23, of New Bedford, daughter of John Wooley, married George W. Newman at Squan Village (N-0279). She appears with George and their sons in Wall in 1880 (N-0096), and in 1900 she was 51, born January 1849, the mother of five children, all living (N-0280). By 1915 she was a widow, heading a Wall household with her son Archibald, her granddaughter Saddie and the infant George G. (N-0288), and in 1920 she owned her home on New Bedford Road with Archibald, Golden and Sadie (N-0097). The family tree gives her death at Neptune on 2 November 1923 (N-0286).
