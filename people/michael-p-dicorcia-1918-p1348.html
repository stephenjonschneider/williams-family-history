What the records say

Michael DiCorcia was born on 18 July 1918 in Jersey City, a twin with his brother Anthony [W-0546, W-0540]. His father Harry was an Italian-born railroad brakeman, and his grandparents Frank and Pauline lived next door [W-0540]. About 1923 the family of eight children moved to Point Pleasant Beach, where they owned a house by 1930 and later lived at 426 River Avenue [W-0541, W-0542]. At 21 he drove a grocery truck; he married Mavis Williams (G3) in 1942 [W-0409] and in 1950 ran his own soda shop with Anthony, living on Trenton Avenue [W-0411, W-0544]. He went on to 35 years with the Postal Service, retiring as Bay Head station manager, served 30 years with Fire Company 2 and ushered at St. Peter's. He died on 1 November 1987 [W-0437, W-0546].
