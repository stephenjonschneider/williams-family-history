What the records say

Daniel Williams first appears on 19 May 1788, when a survey on the Miery Branch was run from the corner of "Daniel Williams's new house (not yet finished)" (W-0021). Later surveys place his house on both sides of the road from Shrewsbury to Squancum (W-0314), and on 7 August 1794 he bought the 30-acre tract from Kenneth Hankinson (W-0022). In 1799 he had "the old tract that he lives on" surveyed and enlarged (W-0022). His father-in-law Aaron Brewer's will of 7 April 1800 leaves £50 to "my daughter Mary Williams" (W-0324). Daniel died without a will before 3 December 1801, when his inventory was taken; Samuel Johnston, husband of his wife's sister, administered the estate (W-0271). In 1832–33 his heirs — Daniel (G7), Catharine Layton and the heirs of Mary Skidmore — divided the homestead (W-0225). Some family trees give 1832 as his death year; that is the year of the heirs' division.
