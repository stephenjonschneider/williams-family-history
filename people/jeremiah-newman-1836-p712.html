What the records say

Jeremiah I. Newmon of Howell made his will on 26 November 1834, 'weak in body', leaving ten acres to his son John Lewis, twenty acres to his son James Holaway, five dollars to his daughter Jane, and the rest to his wife Rebecca while his widow; Jeremiah Newmon Esq. witnessed it, and it was proved on 13 January 1835 (N-0206). Jeremiah Newman Esq.'s 1850 will leaves a share to 'the heirs of Jeremiah Newmon my son' (N-0208).
