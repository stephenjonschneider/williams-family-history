What the records say

A compiled Brouwer family genealogy gives John's baptism on 30 April 1764 in the Dutch Reformed congregations of Freehold and Middletown, as the son of Aaron Brewer and Neeltje Cooper, and says he married Deborah Errickson, had a son Aaron (1794–1872) and applied for a Revolutionary War pension in 1834 [W-0394]. His father's will of 7 April 1800 leaves him the home plantation and all the real estate, with the duty of paying his sisters' legacies, and names him an executor with his stepmother Martha [W-0324]. On 6 January 1801 John, "now in the Township of Shrewsbury", gave the trustees of the Methodist society half an acre beside the meeting house as a burying ground, keeping a share for his own family, on land he held by his father's will [W-0332].
