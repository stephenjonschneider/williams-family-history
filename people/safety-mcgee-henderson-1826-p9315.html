What the records say

His obituary in the White Hall paper records that Safety M. Henderson, son of James and Mary White Henderson, pioneer settlers of Greene County, Illinois, was born on a farm two and a half miles south-east of White Hall on 10 June 1826. He studied at McKendree College, taught school and then farmed 240 acres, and was a Mason for more than fifty years. He married Keziah Williams in March 1854 (eleven children, seven surviving) and, after her death in January 1893, Lucy Gray in June 1896. He died suddenly at a hospital at Asheville, North Carolina, on 20 September 1908 while wintering in the South [BG-0726]. He and his brothers Nathaniel, Thomas, Edwin, James W., Perry and Franklin appear among the portraits of L. P. Allen's Genealogy and History of the Descendants of Mercy Shreve and James White (Greenfield, Illinois, 1897) [BG-0722]. In November 1850, a year after his parents died, he and his brother Franklin were farming with their sister Hope Prentiss and her children north of Apple Creek [BG-0710]; in 1860 he headed his own household near White Hall with his wife Keziah, born in Missouri, and children Luna, Amos, Ellen and Jay C. [BG-0711].
