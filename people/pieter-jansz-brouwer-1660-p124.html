What the records say

Pieter, son of Jan Brouwer and Jannetje Jans, was baptized at the New Amsterdam Reformed Dutch Church on 20 October 1660 [BR-0246, BR-0247]. In 1687 he took the oath of allegiance at Flatlands as a native, and his banns with Annetie Jansen of New Amersfoort were published at Flatbush on 15 February 1687 [BR-0248, BR-0247]. On 30 October 1695 "Piter Brouwer and Antie Brouwers" had their son Hans baptized at Flatbush [BR-0248]. In April 1700 his father conveyed to him his smith's tools and lands [BR-0248]. By 23 December 1706 "Antye Browers" was assessed for his 23 acres [BR-0248].
