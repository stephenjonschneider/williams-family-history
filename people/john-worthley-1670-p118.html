What the records say

John Worthly of Shrewsbury married Elizabeth Hance junior, daughter of John Hance, at the Friends' public meeting house in Shrewsbury on the twelfth of the first month (March) 1695/6 (WO-0187). The Shrewsbury meeting's register lists their children: John (1696), Lydia (1699), Elizabeth (1701), Deborah (1703), Sarah (1706), Joseph (1709), Mary (1710) and Richard, born 22 October 1712, the grandfather of Lydia Borden (G8) (WO-0188). Stillwell's transcription of the register gives his death on the third of the tenth month (December) 1718 (WO-0198); about 1698 he and Elizabeth took in an orphaned Jackson child (WO-0199). His daughter Lydia married George Williams, son of John Williams the Settler (WO-0180, WO-0188).
