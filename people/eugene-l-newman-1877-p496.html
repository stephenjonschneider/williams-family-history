What the records say

Eugene is listed as a son in George W. and Armenia Newman's Wall household in 1880 (N-0096). In 1900 Eugene L., 22, born December 1877, single, was a painter there, like his brother Archibald (N-0280). Find a Grave links Eugene L. Newman as George Wakefield Newman's child (N-0281).
