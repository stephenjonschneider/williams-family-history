What the records say

Abigail Grover married Benjamin Borden, by compiled accounts on 22 Sept 1670 (BG-0271, BG-0528). Her father James Grover's (G13?) will of 1 Dec 1685 left a third of his mill estate to Benjamin for her sake (BG-0286). On 3 May 1690 she and Benjamin sold that third to William Winter (BG-0185), and in 1708 they released it again in the partition (BG-0188). She signed by her mark "A.B." when Benjamin sold his Middletown patent lands to Capt. John Bowne on 7 May 1712 (BG-0321). Stillwell lists ten children born 1672–96, among them Safety Borden and Joseph Borden of Bordentown, and gives her death as 8 Jan 1720, aged 66 (BG-0528).
