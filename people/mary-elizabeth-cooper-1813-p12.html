What the records say

Elizabeth Cooper married Silas Newman (G7) on 27 Nov 1831; the minister's return, by John Saplin Newman, elder in the Methodist Independent Church, and the county index both record it (C-0406, C-0077). In 1850 she was "Elizabeth", 37, in Silas's Howell household with their children Miles, Stewart, Milly, Catharine, Joseph, Silas and Mary (C-0383). The 1855 Howell death return lists their daughter Lydia as a child of "Silas & Elizabeth Newman" (C-0384). She died a widow in Howell on 29 Jul 1882, aged 69 years 1 month; her death certificate says she was born in Monmouth County, lived in New Jersey all her life, and names her parents George and Elizabeth Cooper (G8) (C-0087). Her gravestone reads "Elizabeth Cooper, wife of Silas Newman, June 18. 1813 – July 29. 1882" (C-0405). Her daughter Catherine L. Williams's 1920 death certificate gives her mother's maiden name as Elizabeth Cooper (C-0088).
