What the records say

Anthony DiCorcia was born in Jersey City on 18 July 1918, a twin of Michael (G3 sp.); the twins were listed together as 1 5/12 in January 1920, 12 in 1930 and 21 in 1940 [W-0540–W-0542]. In 1940 he clerked in a retail bakery while living with his parents at 426 River Avenue, Point Pleasant Beach. In January 1946 he and Michael attended St. Peter's Holy Name society together [W-0503], and in 1950, still unmarried, he lived in Michael's household and worked at the fountain of Michael's soda shop [W-0411]. Both parents' obituaries name him as a son of Point Pleasant Beach [W-0436, W-0543].
