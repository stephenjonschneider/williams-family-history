What the records say

Safety Meghee of Bordentown, cordwainer and tanner, left his estate in January 1770 to his five children Amy, William, Mary, Safety and Sarah; one of the witnesses was Thomas White, and Dornan's family account says Amy married Thomas White [BG-0665]. L. P. Allen's 1897 genealogy of the White family makes Amy McGee the second wife of Thomas White (b. 1751), a tanner who moved from Pennsylvania to Ohio about 1798 and died at Chillicothe about 1827; their children, born near Pittsburgh, at McKeesport and at Chillicothe, were Mary (Mrs James Henderson), Ann, Hope (Mrs Walter Van Horn), Samuel, Amy, Eliza (Mrs Levi Reeder) and Safety McGee White, who died in infancy. Amy was living at Columbus, Ohio, in 1839 with her daughter Hope [BG-0705].
