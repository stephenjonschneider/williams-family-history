What the records say

The 1850 census lists Miles Newman, 17, working on the farm in the Howell household of Silas Newman (G7) and Elizabeth (G7); the enumerator marked his sex as female, but his occupation shows a son (N-0220). The 1840 census already shows two boys aged 5–10 in Silas's house (N-0053). In the 1865 state census Miles Newman had his own family in Howell, close to his sister Catherine Louisa (G6) and their widowed mother (N-0273).
