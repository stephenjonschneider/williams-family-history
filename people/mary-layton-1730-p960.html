What the records say

The New Jersey marriage licences record Mary Layton of Middletown and Samuel Bowne, a minor, licensed on 28 Nov 1751, with Christopher Morris as surety (S-0021, S-0074). Her husband was a weaver, adult by 1746, and not the tailor Samuel Bowne who married Patience Conover in 1754, so nothing yet dates her death [S-0281]. Christopher Morris had married Rebecca Layton by licence of 1 May 1742, with Samuel Layton standing surety (S-0074), which makes Mary and Rebecca probably sisters. Stillwell places both women in Samuel's line; the tree puts them under Andrew #4.
