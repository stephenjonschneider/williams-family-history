What the records say

Stillwell records the licence of 12 Sept 1757 for Gershom Cottrell and Catherine Layton, 'daughter of John', with Safety Layton as surety (L-0455, L-0404). Her father John Layton's (G10) will of 1 Mar 1761 names her as Catharine, outside 'my three youngest daughters', so she was the eldest daughter (L-0054). On 4 Nov 1811 she was among the heirs of Hester Cooper (probably her aunt, P128), late of Shrewsbury, who had died seized of a 10 a. lot on Long Brook; the heirs, with G10's grandchildren, appointed James Layton their attorney (L-0088). Stillwell names a son, Joseph Cottrell (L-0404).
