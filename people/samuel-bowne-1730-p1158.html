What the records say

A Samuel Bowne who called himself "minor" - the younger of two living Samuels, not a person under 21 - was a weaver of Middletown and stood surety on a marriage bond in Sept 1746 (BG-0655). As "Samuel Bowne of Middletown minor" he married Mary Layton of Middletown by licence of 28 Nov 1751, with Christopher Morris as surety, signing the bond himself (BG-0053). In Dec 1755 he stood surety for Samuel Layton and Sarah Williams of Freehold, described as a yeoman of Middletown (BG-0054). On 8 March 1756 "Samuel Bowne Mnr" witnessed Esther Bown's administration bond for her late husband Samuel (BG-0055). Reading lists a son Samuel who married Mary Layton among the children of Samuel and Esther (BG-0541).
