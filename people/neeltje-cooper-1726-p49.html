What the records say

Ellenor Neeltje Cooper was the wife of Aaron Brewer (G9). The Brouwer family notes record their son John baptized on 30 April 1764 in the Reformed Dutch congregations of Freehold and Middletown as the son of Aaron Brewer and Neeltje Cooper (C-0025). Her gravestone in the old Bethesda (Adelphia) burial ground gives her death on 22 December 1785 at the age of 59 - the earliest dated stone in that ground; the compiled transcription adds her birth on 19 June 1726 at Middletown and names her parents as Thomas Cooper and Phebe (G10?) (C-0031). Her daughter Mary married Daniel Williams (G8), and Aaron's 1800 will left Mary Williams £50 (C-0037).
