What the records say

On 1 May 1771 Aaron Brewer and his wife Elinor conveyed 160 acres on both sides of the Manasquan at the mouth of Long Brook to James Johnson, as recited in his son James C. Johnson's 1821 mortgage of the same land; a mill lot already sold to John Williams was excepted [W-0378]. In November 1784 Aaron's mortgage to James Davis calls "James Johnson" his son-in-law and refers to the 172 acres Johnson had sold Davis "including where the same now dwells", beside David Knott [W-0328]. James Johnson and Aaron Brewer took the inventory of Daniel Williams of Shrewsbury in 1789 [W-0063]. He witnessed Aaron's will in 1800 and helped take the inventory; the will names his wife Helena [W-0324]. In 1805 James Johnston of Howell mortgaged 160 acres on both sides of the Manasquan at Long Brook to Tunis Forman [W-0236].
