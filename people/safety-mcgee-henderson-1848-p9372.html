What the records say

In September 1850 the household of James W. Henderson, a 33-year-old Ohio-born farmer in Macoupin County, Illinois, included a son Safety, aged 2 [S-0297]. Safety M. Henderson, born in Illinois in May 1848, married Elvira Howard in Harrison County, Iowa, on 27 February 1873 [S-0300], and in 1900 lived at Bedrock, Nez Perce County, Idaho [S-0301]. His gravestone at Pine Hill Cemetery, Southwick, gives his birth on 16 May 1848 and death on 7 December 1916; no Idaho death certificate was found [S-0302].
