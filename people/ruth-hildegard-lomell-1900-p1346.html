What the records say

The Falmouth, Massachusetts, birth register records Ruth's birth on 15 February 1900 to George Lomell, a Swedish-born painter, and Pauline Peterson, born in Norway (W-0421). She was baptized Ruth Hildegard in a Brooklyn church in 1902 (W-0428), and the 1910 and 1920 censuses show her in her parents' Brooklyn household, by 1920 working as a telephone operator (W-0425, W-0434). In 1923 she was in Point Pleasant with her mother and Harry Williams (G4) (W-0422); they married on 14 June 1924 (W-0404). She appears with Harry and their daughters in Lakewood in 1930 and in Brick in 1950 (W-0406, W-0412), and her mother's 1948 death notice names her as Mrs Harry Williams (W-0427). She died at home in Point Pleasant Beach on 8 January 1993, aged 92, after 35 years with New Jersey Bell (W-0423).
