Documented — James Grover (G13?, P01); mother not identified: his father called him "his second son Joseph Grover" in 1678/9 and gave him the Swimming River lands in 1685 (BG-0120, BG-0412).

Spouse

Hannah Nee Lawrence

What the records say

In Feb 1678/9 Joseph Grover, his father's "second son", made a partition agreement with Peter Tilton, witnessed by Thurlough Swiney (BG-0120, BG-0525). He was sworn constable in 1683 (BG-0139) and with his wife Hannah executed Swiney's will in 1684 (BG-0099, BG-0522). His father gave him the Swimming River patent lands on 5 Dec 1685 (BG-0412). In 1688 he bought a share of a propriety and had 480 acres at Crosswicks patented (BG-0420). His will of 7 Dec 1688 left his home land by the Little Falls brook to his son James and the Crosswicks propriety to his daughters, with his wife Hannah and her brother William Lawrence Jr as executors (BG-0523); his inventory, taken 25 Mar 1689, came to £180 5s (BG-0143).
