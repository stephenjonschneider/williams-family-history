What the records say

Her husband Nathaniel Britton had land surveyed on the south side of the North Branch of the Metedeconk in May 1788, his name interlined in 1790, and he carried the chain for Kenneth Hankinson's survey at the Miery Branch in 1789 (L-0309, L-0314). Her father William's (G9) will of 16 Mar 1793 names her as 'Mary Brittin' (L-0057). On 20 Apr 1802 Nathaniel Britton and his wife Mary joined her brother Tunis and sister Catherine Junr, all of Howell, in a quitclaim to their brother William Jr (L-0076). The tree gives her dates as 1780–1855 and a later home at Chapel Hill in Middletown; a birth in 1780 is hard to square with a husband holding land on her family's ground by 1788.
