What the records say

Asher Morse is named in his daughter Harriet's obituary as Asher Morse, Esq., in whose tavern at Ely she was born on 29 December 1805 (WO-0111). A county biography of his grandson Charles H. Wolcott calls him the proprietor of a hotel and farm in Millstone and the owner of a distillery that Charles later ran (WO-0081). A newspaper history of the Ely hotel names him as an early keeper of the Burnt Tavern, which passed to Harriet (G7) (WO-0112). A family tree gives his dates as 1784–1850 and his wife as Elizabeth Britton (WO-0003).
