What the records say

Phebe married Jeremiah Newman Jr on 9 April 1800; his Bible gives her birth as 5 October 1780 and calls her parents Peter (G9) and Phebe (G9) 'my wife's father' and 'my wife's mother' (N-0199). She signed the 1829 release of Peter Newman's land among his heirs (N-0013). The 1850 census shows her at 68 with Jeremiah in Howell (N-0220). Her will of 19 February 1853 leaves her sons Josiah H., Bloomfield and Ferdinand the woodland that was 'my share' of the lands of 'my father Peter Newman' near Shark River and the Long Causeway (N-0200). The Wall return of deaths records Phebe Newman, married, 74, died 19 December 1854, born in Wall, parent Peter Newman (N-0231); the Bible gives a later date, 18 December 1856.
