What the records say

John Newman of Shark River's will of 20 February 1768 leaves his son Joseph £3 and the land where he already lived (N-0202). Shrewsbury tax lists of 1779–86 show a Joseph Newman with 60 to 70 acres, alongside younger Josephs listed as 'son of Jos.' and 'son of Jno.' (N-0026). No other record yet separates his family. The family tree gives him dates of 1718–1788 and a wife Hannah, and proposes him as the father of Peter Newman (G9); no record states that link.
