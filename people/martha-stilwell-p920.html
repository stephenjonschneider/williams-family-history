What the records say

Martha signed with her husband Aaron Brewer when he sold a half share of Great Shopsunk to John Reynolds on 15 June 1792 [W-0388], so she was his wife by then; his first wife Ellenor Neeltje had died in December 1785 [W-0334]. Aaron's will of 7 April 1800 gives Martha all the property she had at the time of their marriage, £50, the largest room in the house with other privileges, and her keep on the place while a widow, and names her an executor with his son John [W-0324]. A compiled Brouwer genealogy also calls her Aaron's second wife [W-0394]. Her maiden name and her later life are not yet found.
