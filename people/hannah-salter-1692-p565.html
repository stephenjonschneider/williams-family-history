What the records say

Hannah Salter was born about 1692, probably at Freehold, the daughter of Richard Salter and Sarah Bowne. By 14 Sept 1714 she had married Mordecai Lincoln Jr, a young ironworker from Hingham, Massachusetts, who had come to Monmouth County; her uncle, the merchant John Bowne, named her as Hannah Lincoln in his 1716 will (BG-0296). Their son John, later "Virginia John", was born at Freehold on 3 May 1716. About 1720 the family moved to the Schuylkill valley in Pennsylvania, where Hannah died about 1727 leaving a son and several daughters (BG-0735).
