What the records say

Jacob Wolcott witnessed his brother Peter's Quaker wedding at Shrewsbury in 1777 (WO-0049). From about 1780 he lived at Mansfield in Burlington County, where he witnessed deeds, worked as a West Jersey deputy surveyor and served as High Sheriff (WO-0093). In 1800 Burlington County trustees conveyed his one-seventh share of his father Benjamin's (G9) land to his sister Meribah and her husband, describing him as 'one of the sons' (WO-0023); a later mortgage refers to the lot James Lafetra 'purchased of Jacob Wolcott' (WO-0045). A family tree gives his death on 17 March 1806 at Mansfield. He was a brother of Amos (G8).
