What the records say

Ellen Peterson is named as the mother of Pauline Peterson in the record of her marriage to George Lomell at Malden, Massachusetts, on 28 February 1892, which gives her parents as Erik and Ellen and her birthplace as Norway [W-0629]. The compiler's family tree adds that she was born in 1848 and died in 1923 [W-0628]. Peterson is presumably her married name; her own surname is not given in either source, and no record yet shows where she lived or died. Pauline's Norwegian baptism is the likeliest paper to give Ellen's maiden name and confirm her dates.
