What the records say

A warrant of 1680 lays out land "for Safety Grover the sonn of James" (BG-0389), and his father's deed of gift and will of 1685 name him a son (BG-0010, BG-0286). He was commissioned ensign of the Middletown foot company on 3 Dec 1683 under Major John Bowne (BG-0662), stood bondsman for his widowed sister Hannah Gardiner in Jan 1687/8 (BG-0663), took patents at the Mill Brook and Shoal Harbour in 1686 (BG-0417), and swore he was 49 in 1707 (BG-0288). With his brother-in-law William Winter he took up Drummond land in 1721, Winter releasing his half in 1722 (BG-0664). In 1733 he signed a bond for his son James (BG-0534); Daniel Herbert's will (1747) mentions gifts "since given" to Herbert's wife Amy Borden by "her honoured Grandfather" - so he was living after their marriage in Oct 1745 (BG-0666). Stillwell and Dornan name his children Hannah (Taylor), James, Esther (Bowne) (G11?), a daughter who married Safety Borden, and Mordecai (BG-0516, BG-0653).
