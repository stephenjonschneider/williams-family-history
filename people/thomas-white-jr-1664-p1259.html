What the records say

Thomas White junior, son of the carpenter, was paid from his father's estate in 1684-86 [WO-0315]. In 1698 he administered the estate of his brother Samuel, who had named him executor [WO-0316]. His own will of November 1712 names his wife Elizabeth, sons Samuel, Thomas, Amos, Jacob and Levi, and daughters Rachel (Mrs Isaac Hance), Leah, Elizabeth and Mary; the farm at Deal and lands on Goose Neck and Long Neck were divided by John Williams, George Curlis, William Brindly and Isaac Hance [WO-0317]. His widow, Elizabeth Cole, later lived at Upper Freehold [WO-0318].
