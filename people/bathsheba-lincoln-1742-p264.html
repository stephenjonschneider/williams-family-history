What the records say

Bathsheba was the wife of Capt. Abraham Lincoln, whom she married in Virginia about 1770, and the mother of his five children, among them Thomas. Widowed in Kentucky in 1786, she raised the family there and lived until 1836. Many trees call her Bathsheba Herring, but Lincoln scholars note that no record of her time gives that name; the online ahnentafel's "Bathsheba Lincoln" is her married name (BG-0735).
