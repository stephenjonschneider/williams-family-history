What the records say

Jonathan Stout had 30 acres surveyed on Hop River in 1686 and patented in 1688 (BG-0441, BG-0447). On 4 Jan 1687/8 his father gave "my loving sone Jonathan Stoutt of Midletoun, farmer" more Hop River land and Conesconck meadow, with John and James Bowne as witnesses (BG-0443). He served on the grand jury in 1689 (BG-0115) and on 27 Feb 1695/6 appraised the estate of his brother-in-law James Bowne (G12?) (BG-0294). His father's will of 1703 names him (BG-0310). The Calendar of Wills records him later as of Hopewell, with a will of 24 Nov 1722 proved 25 Mar 1723 (BG-0311).
