What the records say

Henry Hone married Rebecca Brown in Newton Township, Muskingum County, Ohio, on 5 October 1821 [S-0296]. He was in Union Township, Morgan County, in 1830 and in Washington Township, Hocking County, from 1850; the 1860 census gives his birth in Pennsylvania and Rebecca's in Ireland [S-0298]. His grave at Fairview Church gives his birth on 16 February 1795 and death on 8 February 1866 [S-0299]. Peter Hone and his wife Mary were living in Washington County, Pennsylvania, when he was born [S-0289]. Find a Grave names his children Safety M., Ruth Ann (Barton), Mary Ann (Iles), Amy (Strawn) and James Brown Hone [S-0267; S-0268].
