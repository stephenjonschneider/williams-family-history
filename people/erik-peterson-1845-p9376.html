What the records say

Erik Peterson is named as the father of Pauline Peterson in the record of her marriage to George Lomell at Malden, Massachusetts, on 28 February 1892, which gives her parents as Erik and Ellen and her birthplace as Norway [W-0629]. The compiler's family tree adds that he was born in 1845 [W-0628]. Pauline was born in Norway in April 1867, so the family was presumably Norwegian, but no record yet shows where Erik was born, lived or died, or whether he ever came to America. Pauline's Norwegian baptism and her emigration record are the likeliest papers to give his full name, farm and dates.
