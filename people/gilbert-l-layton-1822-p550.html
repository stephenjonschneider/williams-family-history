What the records say

The Howell census taken on 24 Aug 1850 lists Gilbert Layton, 28, farmer, with real estate worth $800, with Elizabeth (G7), her Williams children including Daniel D. (G6), and their daughter Elizabeth, six months old (L-0292). That August the commissioners Ananiah Gifford, Silas Newman (G7, Newman side) and John Runyon reported on, and then deeded to him, Lot 1 of the late Daniel Williams's (G7) land, the 31.63 a. where the house stands (L-0397, L-0398). On 28 Sept 1852 he and Elizabeth, both signing by mark, released their rights in Lot 5 to James E. Cooper (L-0400), and the next day he deeded the house lot to his daughter Elizabeth (L-0399). The 1865 census shows him in Howell with Elizabeth (L-0418); in 1866 Gilbert L. Layton was elected a trustee of Bethel Methodist Episcopal Church (L-0401); and the 1870 census again shows them together (L-0419). He outlived Elizabeth by eleven years: 'formerly of Bethel', he died alone at his home near Brewers Mill in Jackson Township on 14 June 1892, of blood poisoning, and was buried from Hope church (L-0594).
