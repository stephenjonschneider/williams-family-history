What the records say

Mary appears only as the wife of Thomas Williams (G11) of Freehold, consenting by her own hand and seal when he sold land: on 12 May 1720 (W-0009), on 1 April 1729, when they sold to Hendrick Suydam of Flatbush (W-0010), and on 5 September 1730, when Thomas sold a tract to John Williams, carpenter (W-0001). She also appears in the April 1730 record of the purchase near Crosswicks (W-0011). She is not named in Thomas's sale of June 1737 (W-0012), and no widow appears when his estate was administered in 1746 (W-0062). No record names her family or her children.
