Strong — William Bowne (G13?, P96) & Ann (G13?, P104): the Salem church's own register records "Andrew, son of William Bound", baptized 12:6 [Aug] 1637 (compiled: 1638) (BG-0742); compiled genealogies agree (BG-0307, BG-0292).

What the records say

Compiled histories record Andrew's baptism at Salem in 1638 (BG-0292). In 1684, as Andrew Bowne "of N. Y., merchant", he joined in the administration bond on his brother John's estate (BG-0293). From 1685 to 1697 he took acknowledgments as a Middletown justice, including the Grover gifts of 1685/6 and Obadiah Bowne's sale to Gershom Mott in 1697 (BG-0342, BG-0314), and in 1696 he was fellow bondsman for his nephew James on James Bowne's estate (BG-0294). His will of 6 May 1707, proved 26 June 1708, left everything to his wife and named granddaughters Ann and Lydia Bowne (BG-0295). In 1724 Obadiah Bowne's children called themselves "the only surviving heirs of Andrew Bowne and Elisabeth his wife" (BG-0320).
