What the records say

The Friends' register records John's birth on 22 March 1780, a son of Peter and Lydia Wolcott (WO-0051, WO-0092, WO-0144). With his brother Joseph he administered their father's estate in 1816 (WO-0055, WO-0116) and shared in the 1820 division (WO-0118). His gravestone in the Friends' burial ground gives his death on 26 March 1826 (WO-0053). In 1829 his widow Ann became guardian of their minor children John, Elizabeth, Robinson, Lewis and David (WO-0121).
