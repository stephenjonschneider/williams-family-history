What the records say

John Layton's (G10) will of 1 Mar 1761 names John among the sons (L-0054). He made his mark as bondsman for his brother Andrew's marriage in Dec 1761 (L-0486), and on 10 Feb 1764 John Layton of Shrewsbury, by mark, with Andrew, gave a bond for his own licence to marry Jane Johnston of Freehold (L-0489). On 2 Apr 1766 John Layton of Shrewsbury, yeoman, mortgaged 101 a., his share of his father's land by the Mill Brook beside George Rhea, to Isaac Vandorn (L-0223). He appears in the Shrewsbury tax lists (L-0345), and in Oct 1791 James Layton assigned him a bond of Lydia Ellison (L-0056). On 1 Apr 1801 John Layton of Howell, with the consent of Jane his wife, mortgaged 110 a. on the Howell–Freehold line to Jonathan Forman (L-0232). He was dead by 13 Mar 1809 (L-0236). Mortgages of 1817 and 1818 describe 'the farm that was John Layton's, father of the said Safety', then held by Peter and by Safety Layton (L-0246, L-0247).
