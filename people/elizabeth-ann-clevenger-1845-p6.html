What the records say

Elizabeth Ann Clevenger is named as the mother of Mary Ann Wolcott (G5) on Mary Ann's 1944 death certificate, which gives Elizabeth's birthplace as Smithburg; Mary Ann's father was Amos A. Wolcott (G6), and the two were not married (WO-0001). The 1930 census of her daughter's Lakewood household gives the mother's birthplace as Delaware instead; the death certificate is preferred until her own records are found. A family tree gives her dates as 20 October 1845 to 13 January 1918, her death at West Point Pleasant, and her parents as John Rand Clevenger and Sarah Ann Sygars (WO-0003, WO-0015). The tree also names her son John Caleb Butler Clevenger, who kept her surname (WO-0015). An annotated map marks Smithburg as the family's home (WO-0080).
