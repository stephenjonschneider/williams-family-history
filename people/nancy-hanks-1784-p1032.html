What the records say

Nancy Hanks was born on 5 Feb 1784 in Virginia - the National Park Service gives Hampshire County (now Mineral County, West Virginia); other accounts say Campbell County - and was taken to Kentucky as an infant. She married Thomas Lincoln on 12 June 1806 and was the mother of Sarah, Abraham and Thomas Jr. She died of milk sickness at Little Pigeon Creek, Indiana, on 5 Oct 1818, when Abraham was nine (BG-0735). She is not a Bowne descendant; the Bowne line runs through Thomas.
