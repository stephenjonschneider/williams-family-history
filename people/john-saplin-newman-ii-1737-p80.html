What the records say

As 'John Newman Junr' of Shrewsbury he sold two tracts at Brushy Neck to Samuel Hays in May 1762 (N-0151). John Newman of Shark River's 1768 will makes a son John his residuary heir and executor (N-0202), and Emanuel Newman's 1795 will leaves shares to 'Joseph, Jeremiah and John, sons of brother John' (N-0212). Between 1812 and 1820, as 'John Newman Senr' with his wife Mary, he sold pieces of his Shark River land to Jeremiah Newman Esq. and his sons, and he acknowledged one deed in person on 28 April 1820 (N-0176, N-0190, N-0241). Jeremiah Esq.'s 1854 death return names his parents as 'Jno & Mary Newman' (N-0231). The Squankum John Newman, whose 1782 will names a wife Alice, was a different man (N-0240).
