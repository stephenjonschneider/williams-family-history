What the records say

William P. DiCorcia was born in New Jersey about 1920; he is not in the January 1920 census, so he was born later that year or in 1921 by the stated ages. He was 10 at Point Pleasant Beach in 1930 and, at 19 in 1940, worked as a parking-lot attendant while living with his parents at 426 River Avenue [W-0541, W-0542]. In May 1945 his mother's obituary placed him with the U.S. Army in France [W-0436]. His father's 1950 obituary lists him among the sons living in Point Pleasant Beach [W-0543], and he was living in Point Pleasant when his brother Michael died in 1987 [W-0437].
