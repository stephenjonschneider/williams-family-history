What the records say

Benjamin Wolcott Jr witnessed his brother Peter's 1777 wedding and his sister Meribah's in 1792 (WO-0049, WO-0050). In 1796 he and his wife Anne sold a lot beside Jacob Brown's (WO-0021). As a blacksmith he mortgaged land to Thomas White in 1797 and to Jacob Brown in 1798 (WO-0145, WO-0040), and he witnessed a neighbour's deed in 1798 (WO-0152). Surveys of 1801–03 record his land at Miery Run on Jumping Brook (WO-0094). A 1806 deed recites his father's will leaving a lot to 'Peter and Benjamin' (WO-0029). In 1813 he mortgaged land next to his own dwelling (WO-0047). Tax lists carry him from 1779 to 1808 (WO-0114). Administration on the estate of a Benjamin Wolcott of Monmouth was granted in August 1823, probably his (WO-0056). He was a brother of Amos (G8).
