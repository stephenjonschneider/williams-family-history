What the records say

The Burlington Friends register records the birth of 'Benjamin ye son of Peter & Jone Woolcot' on 20 May 1687, with the midwife Elizabeth Gardiner as a witness (WO-0089). His father, Peter (G11?), died at Burlington in 1690 (WO-0090), and in 1695 his mother Joane married William Worth of East Jersey; a 'Benj: Woolcot' signed among her relations (WO-0138). A Benjamin Wollcott and his wife Comfort of Shrewsbury are named as the parents of Susanna at her marriage in 1743 (WO-0127); whether that is this Benjamin is not proven. Family trees and compilations make him the father of Benjamin (G9) and the husband of a Meribah (WO-0004, WO-0075).
