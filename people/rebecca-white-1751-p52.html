What the records say

Rebecca is known from one record: the Bible kept by her son Jeremiah Newman Jr, which calls her 'my mother' and records her birth on 11 December 1751 and her death on 19 January 1809, aged 58 (N-0199). The same page names her husband, John Newman (1762–1830), as Jeremiah's father, and records Jeremiah's own birth on 10 June 1780. The family tree gives her the surname White; no record yet supports it. She would be the mother of Joseph I. Newman (G8) only if Joseph was John's son by her (Model B).
