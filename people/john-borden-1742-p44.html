What the records say

John Borden was born in the seventh month (September) of 1742, a son of John and Elizabeth Borden of Shrewsbury; the meeting's register lists him with his sisters and brother Mary, Joel, Elizabeth, Anne, Jean, Hannah and Lydia (WO-0190). His father's will of 1768 left him fifteen acres off the home plantation and made him an executor (WO-0179). He was licensed on 28 January 1767 to marry Lydia Worthley of Monmouth, the same day as his sister Elizabeth and John Corlies (WO-0178, WO-0195). The NYGBR lists their daughter Lydia, born 8 February 1773, who married Amos Wolcott (WO-0182). He witnessed his father-in-law Richard Worthley's will in 1784 (WO-0186) and died on 30 January 1836 (WO-0180).
