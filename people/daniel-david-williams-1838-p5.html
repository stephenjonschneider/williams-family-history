What the records say

In 1849 the Orphans' Court named Daniel D. Williams as one of the nine heirs of his father, Daniel Williams (G7) (W-0230), and the 1850 census shows him aged 12 with his widowed mother, Elizabeth Layton (G7), and her second husband Gilbert Layton (W-0232). He served in Co. K, 29th New Jersey Infantry, in 1862–63 (W-0316). The 1865 state census places him in Howell with his wife Catherine Louisa Newman (G6) and son Thomas E. (G5), next to her mother Elizabeth Newman (W-0396). He was a huckster in 1870 and 1880 (W-0397, W-0399) and a produce peddler in 1900 (W-0400). His wife's 1920 obituary names their children Thomas E., Mrs J. H. Clayton and Mrs William A. Cooper (W-0403). In 1930 he lived in Thomas's Lakewood household with four generations of the family (W-0406). He died on 1 March 1937 at his daughter Mrs Cooper's home in Neptune and is buried at Southard (W-0319, W-0407, W-0408). He married Catherine Louisa Newman at the Methodist parsonage in Farmingdale on 7 September 1862 [W-0485]. Newspapers show him buying land in Howell in the 1880s, living briefly at Asbury Park in 1893 and on the Kent road at Southard by 1906 [W-0486, W-0487]. Two of his sons died young: Daniel A. of consumption in March 1897 and Silas N. of a pistol wound in March 1898, when the family lived at North Lakewood [W-0490].
