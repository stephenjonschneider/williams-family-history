What the records say

Esther (G11?) gave Middletown land with her husband Samuel Bowne (G11?) to their son Samuel in 1750 and 1754 (BG-0519). On 8 March 1756 she took administration of Samuel's estate as "Esther Bown widdow and relict of Samuel Bown", signing by mark, with "Capt. James Grover ... Gent." as bondsman (BG-0055). James Grover's will of 6 July 1780 left a third of his lands to "Samuel Bowne son of Samuel deceased of Middletown" and part of the residue to Deborah Patterson - Esther's son and daughter - beside the Taylor grandsons of his sister Hannah (BG-0678). She named a son Safety (BG-0061): by the Safety rule, every traced Safety descends from Safety Grover (G12?) through a daughter, and the Bownes had no Safety of their own. Stillwell and Dornan make her Safety Grover's daughter (BG-0531, BG-0653).
