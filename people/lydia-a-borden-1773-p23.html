What the records say

Lydia, wife of Amos Wolcott (G8), signed by her mark when she and Amos sold part of his father's home lot in 1800 (WO-0024). A family compilation says she was Lydia Borden, married Amos in 1794, and was living in 1850 in Ocean Township with her daughter Jane (WO-0075). Family trees give her birth as 8 February 1773 and her parents as John Borden and Lydia Worthley (WO-0003, WO-0004); a tree's 1850 death in Texas belongs to another woman of the same name. Her son Henry H. Wolcott (G7) is listed among her children in two compilations (WO-0075, WO-0081).
