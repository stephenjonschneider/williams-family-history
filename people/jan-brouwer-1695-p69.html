Parents

Pieter Jansz Brouwer & Susanna Barentse (proof: Compiled)

Compiled — Pieter Jansz Brouwer (G11, P38) & Annetje Jans (G11, P58): no baptism for Jan has been found. He is placed as their son by the NYG&B 2007 compilers and Chester. With his wife Hilletje he sponsored the 1731 baptism of Jan, son of Hans Brouwer, who was Pieter & Annetje's son bpt 1695. He named a son Pieter (1727), and his sister Lucretia (Luyster) and brother Hans also settled in Monmouth (BR-0248, BR-0247). A rival placement makes him a son of Pieter's brother Dirck (BR-0247; Q21).

What the records say

In September 1719 Jan Brouwer had his earmark recorded at Middletown [BR-0134]. With his wife, written "(blank) Van Kleve", "Helena Van Cleve" and "Hilletie" or "Hilletje Van Kleef", he had children baptized at the Freehold and Middletown Dutch congregations: unnamed children on 21 June 1724 and 11 April 1726 (Isbrant Van Cleve and Janneke his wife sponsored the second), Pieter in August 1727, Henrikus in December 1735 and Benjamin on 19 February 1738 [BR-0249]. On 28 November 1731 he and "Hilletie Van Cleefe, his wife" sponsored Jan, son of Hans Brouwer and Nelke Goulder [BR-0248]. No baptism, will or estate has been found for him [BR-0248, BR-0249].
