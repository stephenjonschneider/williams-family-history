What the records say

Francis Borden of Shrewsbury, son of the Francis Borden whose will was made in 1703 (WO-0183), made his own will on 3 February 1753. He named his wife Mary and his children Francis, Thomas, John, Elizabeth, Jean and Amy; Richard, Joseph and Benjamin Borden witnessed, and the will was proved on 6 April 1759 (WO-0184). His son John is the best candidate so far for John Borden of Shrewsbury (died 1768-72), the grandfather of Lydia Borden (G8) who married Amos Wolcott: that John was of the right age, and the names Elizabeth and Jane recur in his family (WO-0179). Nothing yet ties the two Johns together, so this remains a hypothesis.
