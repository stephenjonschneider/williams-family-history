What the records say

The Middletown town book lists William Layton at home lot 14 on 30 Dec 1667 (L-0408, L-0409), and the Portland Point order of 1670 names him among the townsmen (L-0001). In Oct 1676 Anthony Page sold him town lot 12, and he received a headright warrant for 240 a. 'in right of himself & his wife' (L-0002, L-0003). Vauquellin's survey of May 1677 and the patent of 4 June 1677 confirm 218 a.: two house lots, land by the Poplar Field and meadows at Wakecake (L-0004, L-0005); a further 100 a. followed in 1685–86 (L-0013, L-0014). He served on a jury in 1677 and on grand juries in 1689–90 beside John Williams (L-0008, L-0017, L-0018), and was a constituent member of the Middletown Baptist church in 1688 (L-0411). His will of 17–18 Aug 1702 names his wife Violet and children William, Thomas, Andrew, John, Samuel and Mary (L-0022, L-0413). He was dead by 28 June 1708 (L-0022).
