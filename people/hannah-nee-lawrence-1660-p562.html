What the records say

Hannah, widow of Joseph Grover, married Nathaniel Leonard of Middletown on 9 Jan 1694/5 (S-0077). In Mar 1695 the court placed her children by Joseph with their grandfather William Lawrence Sr and named James Grover Senior and Capt. Safety Grover as their nearest relations (S-0078).
