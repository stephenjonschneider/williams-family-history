What the records say

John Lincoln was born at Freehold, Monmouth County, on 3 May 1716, the only son of Mordecai Lincoln Jr and Hannah Salter, and so a great-grandson of Capt. John Bowne. He grew up in Pennsylvania after the family moved there about 1720. In 1743 he married Rebecca (Flowers) Morris, a widow, and about 1768 took his family to Linville Creek in the Shenandoah Valley of Virginia, which earned him the name "Virginia John". He died there in Nov 1788 (BG-0735).
