What the records say

Zebedee Newman first appears as a single freeholder in Shrewsbury tax lists of 1789–95 (N-0026). In 1793 he was a sworn chain bearer at Peter Newman's survey near Three Corner Pond (N-0029), and in 1795 he bought land beside Peter's (N-0154). He married Mary, daughter of Thomas Brand Sr, and from 1814 he gathered the Brand division lots on the South Branch of Wreck Pond (N-0041, N-0175, N-0181). He was co-administrator of Peter Newman's estate from 1828 and signed the 1829 heirs' release (N-0020, N-0013, N-0021). In the 1830s he sold parts of his land (N-0034, N-0035, N-0037), and in 1844 he settled land on his daughter Lydia Alger (N-0033). His will of 31 March 1847 leaves his homestead to his wife Mary and names his son David as executor (N-0031).
