What the records say

On 7 Sept 1744 Amy McGhee, the widow, swore to the inventory of her husband William McGee of Allentown, a brewer, taken on 13 Aug 1744 (BG-0561). Stillwell's Borden account lists Amey as Safety Borden's daughter, married three times (BG-0528); his Grover account gives her children by William Maghee as Safety, James, Catharine and William, though it dates William's death to 1734 (BG-0531). She married Daniel Herbert by licence of 2 Oct 1745 with her father's consent, and his will of 7 Aug 1747 names "his wife's honored grandfather, Safety Grover"; she then married James Mott (BG-0531, BG-0497). Her father's will of 1757 names his grandsons Safty and James Meghee (BG-0560).
