What the records say

Ruth Lomell's Falmouth birth record of February 1900 names her mother as Pauline "Peterson" Lomell, born in Norway (W-0421). The 1900 census gives Pauline's birth as April 1867 (W-0431), and as Paulina Lomell she is named in the Brooklyn baptisms of Ruth, Gertrude and Richard in 1902–03 (W-0428, W-0429). In 1910 she had borne four children, three living (W-0425). She travelled to New York with Ruth and Harry Williams (G4) in August 1923 (W-0422), and in 1930 she lived on Trenton Avenue, Point Pleasant, with George and their sons (W-0426). She died at 829 Trenton Avenue on 19 January 1948; her death notice names George and her children Mrs Edward Williams, Mrs Harry Williams, Richard G. and Leonard G. Lomell, and she was buried at Greenwood Cemetery, Brielle (W-0427). The censuses give her arrival as both 1886 and 1880.
