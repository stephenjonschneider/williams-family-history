What the records say

John Layton's (G10) will of 1 Mar 1761 names Andrew among the sons (L-0054). On 19 Dec 1761 Andrew Laton of Shrewsbury and his brother John gave a £500 bond for Andrew's licence to marry Mary Wilkison of Freehold; Andrew signed 'Andrew Layten', John made his mark (L-0486, L-0414). An Andrew Layton, private in the New Jersey Volunteers, enlisted 6 Apr 1777 and died 9 July 1777, possibly this man (L-0331). He was dead by 4 June 1788, when John Layton, 'eldest son of Andrew Layton of Shrewsbury deceased', released three tracts at Marsh's Bog to his mother Mary (L-0203). Mary's will of 14 Sept 1798 names their children John, Allen, Thomas, Henry, William, Abigail Moore, Hannah Marks, Mary Mathews and Rebecca (L-0293). If he was not the Loyalist who died in 1777, he may be the Andrew who sued William Layton (G9) in 1785 (L-0421).
