What the records say

Frank DiCorcia, the eldest of Harry and Mary DiCorcia's eight children, was born in New Jersey about 1912 and appears as 'Francis', aged 7, in the family's Jersey City household in 1920 [W-0540]. He moved with them to Point Pleasant Beach about 1923 and was 18 and living at home there in 1930 [W-0541]. By 1940 he had left the household. His mother's obituary in 1945 and his father's in 1950 both name him as a son living in Point Pleasant Beach [W-0436, W-0543]. According to the compiler, he later became chief of police of Point Pleasant Beach.
