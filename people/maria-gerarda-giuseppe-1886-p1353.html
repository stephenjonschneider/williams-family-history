What the records say

Mary DiCorcia's obituary in the Asbury Park Press of 26 May 1945 records that she died the previous morning at Point Pleasant hospital, aged 52, after a long illness, and that she lived at 426 River Avenue, Point Pleasant Beach (W-0436). It names her husband Harry DiCorcia (G4), their three daughters (Mrs W. H. Trotter, Mrs George Phillis and Florence), their five sons (Frank, Anthony, Michael (G3), William P. and the Rev. Father Innocent) and her sister, Mrs Jennie Spiccacci of Jersey City. Her son Michael was born in Jersey City in 1918 (W-0437).
