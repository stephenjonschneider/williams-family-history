What the records say

George Lomell, born in Sweden, appears with his wife Pauline in the 1900 census at Falmouth, Massachusetts, as a painter born in March 1868, married ten years, who came to America in 1888 (W-0431). The Falmouth register names him as father of Ruth (G4), born there in February 1900 (W-0421), and a Brooklyn church records the baptisms of Ruth, Gertrude and Richard in 1902–03, the older two born at Malden and Boston (W-0428, W-0429). He was a painter in Brooklyn in 1910 and 1920 (W-0425, W-0434). By 1930 he owned his house on Trenton Avenue, Point Pleasant, working as a decorator in his own business, with his sons Richard and Leonard at home (W-0426). Pauline's 1948 death notice names him as her husband (W-0427), and the New Jersey death index records his death in February 1956 (W-0432).
