What the records say

Lydia Newman joined her husband Joseph I. Newman (G8) in signing the release of Peter Newman's land to Joseph P. Newman on 8 April 1829, among Peter's heirs (N-0013). In 1833 a share of Peter's land near Polly Pod Bog was described as set off 'for Joseph I. Newman and wife' (N-0035). After Joseph's death, letters of administration were granted on 29 September 1847 to Lydia and Silas Newman (G7) together (N-0015). Silas signed his 1847 release as heir at law (N-0008), and by December 1849 he was called the surviving administrator (N-0017). She does not appear in the 1850 census. The 1830 and 1840 censuses show a woman of 50–60 and then 60–70 in a household that may be hers (N-0052, N-0053).
