What the records say

A Mary Williams witnessed the deed of 1 March 1800 by which her father Aaron Brewer and his wife Martha sold six acres by the Methodist meeting-house lot to Peter Brewer (BR-0075). Five weeks later Aaron's will of 7 April 1800 names "my daughter Mary Williams", with a legacy of £50 (BR-0022). The estate papers of her husband, Daniel Williams (G8), who died before 3 December 1801, list that legacy, and his administrator was Samuel Johnston, the husband of her sister Anna (BR-0027). When Daniel's heirs divided the Miery Branch homestead in 1832–33 they were her children: Daniel (G7), Catharine (Mrs Aaron Layton) and the heirs of Mary Skidmore (BR-0041). Her mother, Ellenor Neeltje Cooper (G9), is buried at Adelphia (BR-0012). No record yet gives Mary's own birth or death.
