What the records say

Luetta Mae Williams was a daughter of Daniel D. Williams and Catherine Louisa (Newman) Williams (G6) and a sister of Thomas E. Williams (G5). Her mother's obituary of March 1920 names her as "Mrs. William A. Cooper", one of three surviving children (C-0089). Her father, a Civil War veteran aged 99, died on 1 March 1937 at her home, 1315 Seventh Avenue, Neptune; his obituary names her as his daughter, and his death certificate names his wife Catherine L. Newman (C-0091, C-0090). She was a Cooper only by marriage and is not known to descend from George Cooper (G8).
