What the records say

The Middletown Town Book records James Bowne's (G12?) earmark on 4 Jan 1679 as "recorded to his son Samuel Bowne", so Samuel was born before then (BG-0304). He was a juror in 1721 and 1735 (BG-0042, BG-0088) and owed a bond with Samuel Legg to William Covenhoven's estate in 1742 (BG-0648). In October 1744 he gave written consent for "John Pattonson" to marry his daughter (Deborah) (BG-0669). With his wife Esther (G11?) he gave land to their son Samuel in 1750 and 1754 (BG-0519). He died without a will; Esther, signing by mark, took administration on 8 March 1756 with "Capt. James Grover, Gent." as bondsman, witnessed by "Samuel Bowne Mnr" and Elizabeth Woodward (BG-0055). His sons Safety and Samuel arbitrated in 1767 (BG-0061).
