What the records say

Francesco 'Frank' DiCorcia was born in Italy in July 1855 [W-0548]. A stone mason, he crossed to America more than once in the 1880s and 1890s — once bound for Scranton, Pennsylvania [W-0552] — before settling in Jersey City, where his wife Pasqualina and their children joined him around 1898-1901 [W-0547]. By 1910 he owned a house on Second Street; his son Donato 'Harry' and the rest of the family lived with or beside him through 1920, when his grandson Michael was a toddler next door [W-0540, W-0548]. His parents, per the family tree, were Donato DiCorcia and Catarina DiLuca [W-0554].
