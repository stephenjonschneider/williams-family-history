What the records say

A family Bible records Annie Strickland's marriage to Amos A. Wolcott (G6) on 22 June 1865, and her parents Barkalow Strickland and Abbie Bennett's in 1833 (WO-0078). Her father's 1872 will, proved in 1873, leaves 'Ann Wolcott' part of the Harmony tract in Jackson Township (WO-0059). The 1870 census finds her keeping house at Keyport with Amos and their daughter, then called Annie A. (WO-0084); in 1880 she is there with Augusta and Telfair (WO-0085). In 1900 she was a dressmaker in Gainesville, Florida, aged 60, born March 1840, married 35 years, the mother of two living children (WO-0086). Amos's 1908 obituary names her as his late wife (WO-0076).
