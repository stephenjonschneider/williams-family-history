What the records say

The 1850 census lists Mary, 34, as the wife in Francis Newman's Howell household, with eight children from Huldah, 18, to Ruth, eight months (N-0094). In 1870 Mary A. Newman, 54, lived with Francis in Wall, with William, George W. and Hampton, next door to Mary Bawmer, 52, a seamstress (N-0095). Find a Grave lists Mary Ann Newman as the mother of George Wakefield Newman (N-0281). The family tree gives her maiden name as Bearmore, her birth on 4 April 1816, her marriage to Francis on 9 November 1831 and her death on 22 November 1880 (N-0101, N-0286).
