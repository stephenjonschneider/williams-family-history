What the records say

Daniel DiCorcia was born in New Jersey about 1916. He was 3½ in the family's Jersey City household in January 1920 and 13 at Point Pleasant Beach in 1930 [W-0540, W-0541], but had left home by 1940. He became a Catholic priest under the religious name Father Innocent. His mother's 1945 obituary lists 'the Rev. Father Innocent, Rhode Island' among her five sons, and his father's in 1950 'the Rev. Innocent DiCorcia, Trenton' [W-0436, W-0543]. The other four sons are named in those notices as Frank, Michael, Anthony and William, so Father Innocent must be Daniel.
