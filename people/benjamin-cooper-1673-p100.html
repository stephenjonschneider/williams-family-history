What the records say

Benjamin Cooper married Helena Wilkins in New York on 7 April 1694 (C-0307). On Staten Island he registered a cattle mark in 1698, was chosen assessor of the west division in 1704, and appears in the c. 1706 census with Elener and Martha Coper (C-0308, C-0309, C-0310). In 1712 "Benjamin Cooper, late of the Fresh Kills on Staten Island, now of Middletown" bought 184 acres about the Broad Swamp (C-0004); his land is a boundary in Middletown surveys and releases of 1721–22 (C-0112, C-0113). On 30 May 1744 he deeded land at Shoal Harbour to his son Thomas (G10?) (C-0312). He married Hester Borden on licence of 12 April 1754 (C-0394). His will of 17 May 1755, proved 24 June, names his wife Hester and children Benjamin, Obadiah, David, Josiah, Elizabeth, Phebey and Ellenor (C-0318). In 1767 his 1744 bond was sued against Ezekiel Cooper, son and heir of John Cooper, Benjamin's heir (C-0123).
