What the records say

The 1910 census of South Shrewsbury lists Sadie as the daughter of Archibald Newman, a carriage painter, and his wife Alice, who had borne one child, then living (N-0287); her age there is written 8/12, while later records place her birth in November 1901. In 1915 'Saddie', born November 1901, was at school and living in Wall with her widowed father, her grandmother Armenia and the infant George G. (N-0288). In 1920 she was 18, a bench worker, in Armenia's household (N-0097). On Saturday 27 October 1923 she married Vernon Anderson of Point Pleasant at the Belford Methodist parsonage (N-0283). The 1940 census shows the couple in Point Pleasant with Alice, Betty, Eileen, Paul and Ruth (N-0098), and in 1950 they lived at Point Pleasant Beach, with Eileen and Richard Harris next door (N-0285). Vernon's 1979 obituary names her as his widow (N-0100).
