What the records say

The 1900 census lists Stella, 12, born February 1888, as the daughter of George W. and Armenia Newman in Wall (N-0280). Find a Grave links a Stella Williams as George Wakefield Newman's child, so she married a Williams (N-0281); the marriage itself has not yet been found.
