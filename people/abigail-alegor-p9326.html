What the records say

Abigail Alegor married Safety Layton on 2 Apr 1829 (S-0093). On 8 May 1840 administration on the estate of "Safty Layton late of the County of Monmouth" was granted to Abigail Layton (S-0068), and the same day his goods were inventoried as those of "Safty Layton late of Howell", with Jeremiah Newman and Cornelius Allgor as appraisers (S-0127). Her daughter Mary Bennett's death certificate of 1889 names the mother as "Abby" (S-0131).
