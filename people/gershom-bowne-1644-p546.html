What the records say

Gershom Bowne received a Proprietors' patent on 26 July 1686 for land by the old Middletown path (BG-0327), and in 1688 his land lay south of Lydia Bowne's 500 acres at Mowhingsinge (BG-0391). About 1699 it bounded Obadiah Bowne's survey (BG-0401), and by 10 Mar 1704/5 deeds speak of "the land of Gersham Bowne deceased" (BG-0351). On 20 Sept 1709 his son and heir William Bowne, yeoman, sold 78 acres of the patent (BG-0327); a 1715 survey still calls it land "late of Gersham Bown, deceased" (BG-0225). No record names his parents.
