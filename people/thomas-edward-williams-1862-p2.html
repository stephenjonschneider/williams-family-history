What the records say

The New Jersey birth index records Thomas E. Williams born 30 May 1862 and names his father, Daniel D. Williams (G6) (W-0395). The 1865, 1870 and 1880 censuses show him as a boy in Daniel's Howell household, beside his Newman grandmother (W-0396, W-0397, W-0399). A family marriage certificate records his marriage to Mary A. Wolcott (G5) in December 188? (WO-0002). By 1900 he had his own Howell household as a teamster (W-0400), and in 1910 he was farming on Bennetts Mills Road (W-0402). In 1930 he was a tinsmith on Ridge Avenue, Lakewood, with his father and his son Harry F. (G4) in the same household (W-0406). His father's 1937 obituary names him as a son living in Point Pleasant (W-0319). He died on 18 March 1956 at his daughter's home on Ocean Road, Point Pleasant; his obituary counts 136 descendants (W-0413, W-0414). The family application's 18 April is an error.
