What the records say

Harriet's obituary says she was born on 29 December 1805 in the tavern at Ely kept by her father, Asher Morse (G8), and that she died at the Freehold home of her son Charles H. Wolcott on 14 December 1893, in her 88th year (WO-0111). It names her husband, Henry H. Wolcott (G7), and recalls their years at the Burnt Tavern and at Toms River. The 1850 census shows her at Millstone with Henry and five children, among them Amos (G6) (WO-0083). Henry's will leaves her the household goods and furniture (WO-0060). A county biography of her son Charles and a later newspaper history of the Ely hotel tell how the old Burnt Tavern passed to her from her father (WO-0081, WO-0112). Amos A.'s 1908 obituary names her as his mother (WO-0076).
