What the records say

Tax lists show Peter Newman as a Shrewsbury freeholder with 200 acres, horses and cattle from 1779, and with half a sawmill in 1786–89 (N-0026); in 1780 he was listed as a delinquent militia private (N-0230). In 1788 Andrew Thomas sued him for assault (N-0025). In 1791 and 1793–95 he had surveys made south of Shark River near Polly Pod Bog and Three Corner Pond; at the 1793 survey he was present with 'his son Peter' (N-0027, N-0029). In 1800 Peter and his wife Phebe (G9) sold 10.44 acres to a Joseph Newman (N-0010), and in 1827 he sold land to Joseph P. Newman (N-0023). He died without a will; letters went to Jeremiah Newman Jr and Zebedee Newman on 10 September 1828 (N-0020), and his heirs released his land in 1829 (N-0013). The Bible of his son-in-law Jeremiah Jr gives his birth as 11 December 1753 and his death in 1829, aged 76; the 1828 letters show he had died by then (N-0199).
