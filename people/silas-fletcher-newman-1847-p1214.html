What the records say

The 1850 census lists Silas, aged 3, in the Howell household of Silas Newman (G7) and Elizabeth (G7) (N-0220), and the 1865 state census has Silas F. with his widowed mother beside his sister Catherine Louisa (G6) (N-0273). The 1873 county atlas marks 'S.F. Newman' on the North Branch of the Metedeconk below Bethel (N-0303). In 1953 his son Stuart H. Newman of Ford Road, Lakewood, said that his parents, Mr. and Mrs. Silas Fletcher Newman, had owned more than 60 acres of farm land beside his home, and that his grandfather Silas had been a circuit rider (N-0304).
