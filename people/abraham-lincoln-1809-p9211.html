What the records say

Abraham Lincoln, 16th President, was born in Hardin County, Kentucky, on 12 Feb 1809, son of Thomas Lincoln and Nancy Hanks, and died at Washington, D.C., on 15 Apr 1865 after being shot. His father's line runs back through Capt. Abraham Lincoln and "Virginia John" Lincoln - born at Freehold, Monmouth County, in 1716 - to Hannah Salter and her mother Sarah Bowne, daughter of Capt. John Bowne of Middletown (BG-0735, BG-0296). Capt. John was the brother of Judge James Bowne (G12?), so the President shares William Bowne and Ann (G13?) with the compiler: a 6th cousin 5 times removed. Lea's Ancestry of Abraham Lincoln (1909) is held as BG-0292.
