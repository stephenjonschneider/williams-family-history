What the records say

The Methodist preachers' register follows Safety Layton from the Philadelphia Conference (Salisbury, Snow Hill, Lewes, 1854-56) to Indiana (1857-66), with service as an army chaplain [S-0130] - the Indiana roster dates it: chaplain of the 9th Indiana Infantry, of Logansport, commissioned 5 September 1861, resigned 6 July 1862 [S-0187]. He married Sarah H. Craft in Wayne County, Indiana, on 2 November 1858 [S-0181], and the 1860 census lists him at Centreville as a Methodist clergyman, 29, born in New Jersey, with Sarah and an infant daughter [S-0182]. In 1867 he administered John H. Herbert's estate at Logansport [S-0171]. In 1869 he bought 40 acres of railroad land in Cook County [S-0183], and in 1870 he was an attorney in Chicago worth $25,000 [S-0184]. A Safety Layton of Kansas City conveyed 5,000 acres in Missouri in 1880 [S-0169]. He died intestate at Chicago about 21 July 1882, leaving under $1,000; a creditor, Henry J. Goodrich, petitioned for administration [S-0189, S-0185].
