What the records say

Hannah Grover was a daughter of Capt. Safety Grover (G12?) and married William Taylor (1688-1767), son of Edward Taylor I of Middletown. William's will, proved 12 Oct 1767, names his wife Hannah and his "brother-in-law, James Grover" - Safety Grover's son (BG-0532, BG-0696). The Grover typescript lists their children as Safety, Edward, Mercy, Deborah, Hannah, Catherine, Martha, Rebecca and Esther (BG-0696); Safety Taylor died in November 1766 (gravestone, BG-0695). Her brother James Grover's will of 1780 remembered her grandsons Grover, John and Edward Taylor beside her sister Esther's children (BG-0678). Like Esther, she named a son Safety and daughters Catherine and Esther.
