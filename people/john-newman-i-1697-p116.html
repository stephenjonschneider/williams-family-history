What the records say

From 1742 to 1766 the proprietors' surveyors used John Newman's land south of Shark River as a boundary. A 1746 Brinley survey begins at 'a little brook that runs thro' John Newman's land into Shark River' (N-0118), and Brinley, Forman and Knott surveys of 1742–66 name him as an adjoiner (N-0064, N-0065, N-0066, N-0067, N-0068). His will of 20 February 1768, proved 2 May 1768, calls him John Newman of Shark River, Shrewsbury Township. It gives his wife Deliverance her living out of the estate and his son Joseph the land where he lives; it names sons Emanuel, David, Thomas and Samuel and daughters Catherine Croxon and Mary Dunigan; and it leaves the rest to his son John, executor with Deliverance (N-0202). Deliverance is known only from this will. This row covers both husband and wife (G11).
