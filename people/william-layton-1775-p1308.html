What the records say

His father William's (G9) will of 16 Mar 1793 asks his wife to raise the two youngest, William and Catherine (L-0057). In 1802 his brothers and sisters quitclaimed to William Layton Jr their rights in land on the North Branch, and his brother Walter sold him a meadow lot there (L-0076, L-0075). He married Mrs Jane Voorhees on 29 Dec 1802 (L-0416). In 1811 he was an heir of Hester Cooper (L-0088). On 23 Mar 1816 William Layton and his wife Jane, he signing by mark, released his share of his father's homestead to his nephew Aaron Layton Jr (L-0113). In 1821 Thomas D. Bowne sold a William Layton of Howell, whose house stood on the line, 50.76 a. running from Aaron Layton's old line (L-0151).
