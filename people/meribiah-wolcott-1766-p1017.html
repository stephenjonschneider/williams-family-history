What the records say

Meribah Wolcott married James Lafetra Jr, a tanner, at Shrewsbury on 12 July 1792; her mother Clementine (G9) and her brothers Henry, Peter, Benjamin and Amos (G8) signed among the bride's family (WO-0050). In 1800 trustees conveyed her brother Jacob's share of their father's land to James and 'Merebah his wife' (WO-0023). She witnessed her nephew Joseph's wedding in 1807 (WO-0052). The Friends' register lists her children, beginning with George Wolcott Lafetra, born in 1793 (WO-0146).
