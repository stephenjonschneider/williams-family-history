What the records say

Lydia Potter married Peter Wolcott at the Shrewsbury meeting house on 26 June 1777; Potters and Wolcotts signed as witnesses (WO-0049). The Friends' register lists their thirteen children (WO-0051, WO-0092, WO-0144). She joined Peter in selling land in 1802 and 1806 (WO-0028, WO-0029) and witnessed his sister Meribah's wedding in 1792 (WO-0050). After Peter's death she renounced the administration to her sons (WO-0055), and in 1820 a third of his land was set off as her dower (WO-0118). She died without a will; in 1828 her daughter Clementine and Clementine's husband complained against the administrator (WO-0119).
