[superseded round 154: "Probable"]

What the records say

Stillwell's no. 19, James Grover, son of Safety Grover (G12?), appears as "James Grover, Jr." in an account book in 1727 and in a bond with Safety in 1733, and became deacon and justice (BG-0534); "Safety Grover & James Grover Junr" owed a joint bond to William Covenhoven's estate in 1742 (BG-0648). In 1756 "Capt. James Grover ... Gent." was bondsman for his sister Esther Bowne's administration (BG-0055). In 1778 he was "son and heir of Safety Grover". His own will of 6 July 1780 - read from the original - left his wife Sarah (Stillwell) a settlement, the Lower place to Patience and Nancey Burdge and then to the Baptist deacons, and his remaining lands in thirds to "Samuel Bowne son of Samuel deceased of Middletown" and Grover and John Taylor, sons of William Taylor of Freehold; Deborah Patterson shared the residue (BG-0678). His inventory, 17 Aug 1780, came to £235 (BG-0678).
