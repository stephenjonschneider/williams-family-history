What the records say

Jeremiah Newman was a Shrewsbury householder by 1784 (N-0026), and in 1793–95 he was 'now dwelling' on the Kinnan–Williams–Longstreet tract at Three Corner Pond (N-0029, N-0272). His uncle Emanuel's will of 1795 names him among the sons of 'brother John' (N-0212), and Joseph Newman's 1831 will calls him 'my brother' (N-0201). As Jeremiah Newman Esq. he was a commissioner for the Thomas Brand division in 1814 (N-0041), bought land south of Shark River and at Squan Beach (N-0183), and witnessed his father's sales to his sons in 1816–20 (N-0241). The 1850 census lists him as a farmer aged 96 (N-0220). His will of 17 September 1850 names sons John, Jeremiah, Abbott and Garret and eight daughters (N-0208). The Wall death return records him, widower, 94, died at Shark River on 31 May 1854, son of 'Jno & Mary Newman' (N-0231).
