What the records say

In 1800 Peter Newman (G9) and his wife Phebe sold 10.44 acres south of Shark River to a Joseph Newman (N-0010), and in 1827 Peter sold three acres to Joseph P. Newman, west of the land Joseph P. had bought from him before (N-0023). On 8 April 1829 Peter's heirs released 18 acres to Joseph P. (N-0013). He died without a will, and letters went to his widow Lydia on 25 May 1841 (N-0205). His son Jesse B. Newman sought the division of his land and the assignment of dower to Lydia in 1843 (N-0045, N-0102, N-0103), naming the heirs Phebe Romine, Blessing Newman, Jane Stout, Jedidah Morris, Uriah and Jesse B. (N-0102, N-0233).
