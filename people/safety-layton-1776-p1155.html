What the records say

His father's will of 1790 names him among "my 3 youngest sons" and orders that he be "put to a trade" (S-0048, S-0117). The later records are of the Safety Layton of Howell who bought small lots beside Robert Hight and John Newman Jr at Polly Pod Bog in 1815–16 and sold in 1821 (S-0052, S-0053, S-0058). He married Mary Alger in 1820 and Abigail Alegor in 1829 (S-0092, S-0093). In 1835 he employed Thomas Hooper and charged him for whiskey and tobacco, and with Thomas Allgor and James Layton he was sued by Hooper in 1837–38 (S-0122, S-0126). He died before 8 May 1840, when Abigail took administration and his tools and a part-built wagon were inventoried (S-0068, S-0127). His daughter Mary Bennett's death certificate names "Safty Layton" and "Abby" (S-0131). A Marsh genealogy says a Safety Layton "of West Jersey" married Hetty Marsh (S-0118).
