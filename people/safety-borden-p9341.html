Safety Borden

Also known as
Safety Borden

Open — a Borden father and (probably) a Cheesman mother, perhaps the daughter Charity; no Person IDs; Dornan's card on the will (BG-0665).

What the records say

Known only from a card among John Pickens Dornan's notes: William Cheesman Sr of New Brunswick made his will on 3 December 1753 (proved 30 January 1759), naming his wife Martha, granddaughters Mary and Martha, daughters of his daughter Charity (perhaps deceased), "and gr. son Safety Borden"; Dornan added "Who was?" [BG-0665]. A William Cheesman of Middletown had a 1688 grant at Navesink bounded by Safety Grover's land.
