What the records say

Archibald is listed as a son in George W. and Armenia Newman's Wall household in 1880 (N-0096), and in 1900 as 26, born December 1873, single, a painter (N-0280). On Sunday 25 April 1909 'Archibald Newman, a son of Mr. and Mrs. George W. Newman of Shrewsbury', married Alice C. Golden, daughter of Joseph A. Golden of Scobeyville, at the Methodist parsonage (N-0282). In 1910 he was a carriage painter on Farmingdale Road, South Shrewsbury, with Alice and their daughter Sadie (N-0287). By June 1915 he was widowed and living in Wall with his mother, Saddie and the infant George G. (N-0288). The 1920 and 1930 censuses show him as a painter, in 1930 with his son Golden (N-0097, N-0284). The 1915 census gives his birth as December 1872.
