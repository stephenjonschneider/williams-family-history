What the records say

Harry F.'s delayed birth certificate records his birth at Southard, Howell, on 30 September 1900 to Thomas E. Williams (G5) and Mary Wolcott (G5) (W-0401). In 1910 he was in his parents' household on Bennetts Mills Road (W-0402). In August 1923 a Point Pleasant social column has him visiting New York with Ruth Lomell (G4) and her mother (W-0422), and they married on 14 June 1924 (W-0404). Their daughter Mavis (G3) was born in Point Pleasant that December (W-0405). In 1930 he worked in sheet metal and lived with Ruth, Mavis and Amada in his father's Lakewood household (W-0406); in 1950 he ran a riding academy in Brick (W-0412). He died on 10 April 1973 at Point Pleasant Hospital (W-0415); his obituary describes his sheet-metal career and names Ruth and their three daughters (W-0416). The obituary gives Lakewood as his birthplace; the birth certificate's Southard is preferred.
