What the records say

William Layton's (G9) will of 16 Mar 1793 names Thomas among his sons, then under 21 (L-0057). On 11 Sept 1795 Thomas Layton had 9.2 a. surveyed on a right of purchase on the main branch of the Metedeconk (L-0318). He married Rebecca Ann Matthews on 2 Oct 1803 (L-0415, L-0416), and in 1804 David Taylor sued him on a single bill (L-0428). In 1811 he was among the heirs of Hester Cooper (L-0088). In Nov 1816, as Thomas Layton of Freehold Twp signing by mark, he partitioned his father's homestead with his nephew Aaron Layton Jr, taking about 21.75 a. (L-0118, L-0119), and with his wife Rebeccah quitclaimed his share to Tunis Layton Jr, reciting 'my father William Layton deceased' (L-0121). He headed a Howell household in 1830 (L-0417). The death certificate of his daughter Elizabeth (G7) names him as her father (L-0221). He is probably the Thomas W. Layton boarded in Freehold in 1839–41 (L-0434).
