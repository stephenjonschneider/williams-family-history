What the records say

The Friends' register records Clementine's birth on 28 August 1784, a daughter of Peter and Lydia Wolcott (WO-0051, WO-0092, WO-0144). She witnessed her parents' gift of a schoolhouse lot in 1802 (WO-0028) and, as Clementine Van Note, her brother Joseph's wedding in 1807 (WO-0052). She shared in her father's land in 1820 (WO-0118), and in 1828 she and her husband James Van Note complained against the administrator of her mother's estate (WO-0119).
