MIDDLETOWN · 1658–1756

Grovers, Bownes and a boy named Safety

James Grover's son Safety was a Middletown militia officer from 1683 and a captain by 1695. He took up land at the Navesink and Shoal Harbour, and in 1685 his father deeded him more as “my son Safety Grover.” James and Mary Bowne's son Samuel married Esther, whom the documents support as Safety's daughter. When Samuel died without a will in 1756, Esther took out the administration on 8 March with Captain James Grover, apparently her brother, as her surety. His will of 6 July 1780 left land to “Samuel Bowne son of Samuel deceased of Middletown” and part of the rest to Samuel's daughter Deborah Patterson, so two of Esther's children were among his heirs. Only a record naming her as a Grover is still missing.

They named a son Safety Bowne. That unusual name is one of the strongest clues in this history. Twenty-four men named Safety have now been traced, from Monmouth to Pennsylvania, Ohio and Illinois, and every one whose line is known descends from James Grover. So far, wherever the name appears, a Grover is behind it.
