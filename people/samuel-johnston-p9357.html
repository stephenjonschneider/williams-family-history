What the records say

Samuel Johnston had a house by May 1786 near Rattlesnake Brook and the Great Swamp, south of the Manasquan, beside the Claytons and Matthews [W-0297]. Aaron Brewer's will of 7 April 1800 names his daughter "Anna Johnston (wife of Samuel Johnston)" [W-0324]. On 14 January 1802 Samuel, signing "Johnson", gave bond with Thomas Emmons and Lewis White as administrator of Daniel Williams (G8), the husband of Anna's sister Mary, and swore that Daniel had died without a will [W-0271]. In May 1836 a Samuel Johnston adjoined and witnessed a survey on Barren Meadow Brook in Howell where an Aaron Brewer was also a witness [W-0320].
