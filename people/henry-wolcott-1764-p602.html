What the records say

Henry Wolcott signed among the family at his sister Meribah's wedding in 1792 (WO-0050). In 1796 he and his wife Abigail sold woodland at the Little Bog, next to land his brother Peter had bought from their father (WO-0022). He bought land from John Bond in 1809 and appears as a neighbour in deeds of 1807–1809 (WO-0035, WO-0031, WO-0034, WO-0037). Tax lists carry him from 1785 to 1808 (WO-0114). His children Elizabeth and Job married in 1814 and 1821 (WO-0132, WO-0133). He died without a will; letters of administration were granted on 28 July 1834 to Henry W. and John Wolcott (WO-0057). In 1835 his widow Abigail's dower was set off and his land divided among his children (WO-0120), and the estate account was settled in 1836 (WO-0154). He was a brother of Amos (G8).
