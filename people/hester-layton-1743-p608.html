What the records say

John Layton's (G10) will of 1 Mar 1761 names a daughter Hester among 'my three youngest daughters', under 18 (L-0054). On 6 Jan 1762 John Cottrell of Freehold and Gershom Cottrell of the same town, yeomen, gave the governor a £500 bond for John's licence to marry Hester Layton of Shrewsbury Township; John signed his name, Gershom - her sister Catherine's husband - made his mark, and Sam Leonard witnessed (L-0488, L-0455). John Cottrell's will later left £5 to 'my two first wives children', James, Thomas, William and John (L-0222, L-0404). She was long taken to be the Hester Cooper whose heirs sold a Long Brook lot in 1811 (L-0088), but an older Esther Layton who became Hester Borden and then, in 1754, Hester Cooper (P128) now fits that woman better (Q74).
