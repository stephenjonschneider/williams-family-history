What the records say

One record names him: on 27 Mar 1833 "Safety Layton Jr" located 1.05 acres on a right of location south of the South Branch of Wreck Pond, with Jesse and Robert E. Newman as chain bearers (S-0064). The "Jr" shows an older Safety Layton was then living. A Safety Layton sued John S. Layton in 1842; that file has not been read.
