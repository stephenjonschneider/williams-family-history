What the records say

Mary (G12?) married James Bowne (G12?) at Gravesend on 26 Dec 1665 (BG-0290, BG-0307). In Nov 1675 the Proprietors' warrants gave her 60 acres in her own right and James 240 acres "in right of him selfe & his wife Mary Stout" (BG-0302, BG-0367). Her husband's estate was appraised in Feb 1696 by her brother Jonathan Stout (BG-0294). Her father Richard Stout's (G13?) will of 9 June 1703 names her among his daughters, so she was then living (BG-0310). The Town Book earmark of 1679 shows her son Samuel (G11?) (BG-0304). An Ancestry tree gives her death as 26 Dec 1675, which is her wedding date mis-copied.
