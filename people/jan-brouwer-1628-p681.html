What the records say

Jan Brouwer and Jannetje Jans had children baptized at the New Amsterdam Reformed Dutch Church, among them Pieter on 20 October 1660 [BR-0246, BR-0247]. Jan was a smith at Flatlands. On 22 and 23 April 1700 he conveyed his smith's tools and his lands to his son Pieter, and acknowledged the conveyances on 17 November 1702 [BR-0248]. The Brewer DNA Project treats his descendants as a Y-DNA line distinct from Adam Brouwer's [BR-0133].
