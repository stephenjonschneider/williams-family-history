What the records say

George, 5, is listed in the 1850 Howell household of Francis and Mary Newman (N-0094), and George W., 25, in their Wall household in 1870 (N-0095). On 25 February 1872 Geo. W. Newman, 26, of New Bedford, son of Francis Newman, farmer, married Armenia Wooley, 23, daughter of John Wooley, at Squan Village (N-0279). The 1880 census shows him farming in Wall with Armenia and sons Archibald, Everett and Eugene (N-0096). In 1900 he was 55, born May 1845, married 28 years, with Armenia, Archibald, Eugene L., Clarence W. and Stella (N-0280). Find a Grave records George Wakefield Newman, 1845–1908, at Glendola Cemetery (N-0281), but the April 1909 notice of his son Archibald's wedding still speaks of 'Mr. and Mrs. George W. Newman of Shrewsbury' (N-0282).
