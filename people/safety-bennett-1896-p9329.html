What the records say

Safety Bennett, a farmer of Allenwood aged 19 and born at Asbury Park, married Luella J. Haley, 18, of Allenwood and born at Hornerstown, on Christmas Day 1914 at 3 p.m., at Corlies Avenue, Asbury Park; his parents were Andrew S. Bennett and Malissa V. Bennett, and the witnesses were Ona and William I. Lane (S-0141). A Freehold newspaper adds that the house was his sister Mrs. William I. Lane's, in West Grove, that Rev. Curwen B. Fisher of the West Grove Methodist church officiated, and that the couple would live at Allenwood, where he farmed (S-0138). A burial index gives his birth on 23 Sep 1896 at Asbury Park and his death on 2 Mar 1976 at Lawrenceville, Mercer County, where he is buried, with his wife Luella (S-0145); a family tree names his children Dorothy, James A., Ethel and Charles (S-0139).
