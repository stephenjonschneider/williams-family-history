What the records say

Francis Borden of Shrewsbury made his will on 24 May 1703, naming his wife Jane, his sons Richard, Thomas and Francis, his daughter Joyce, wife of John Hance Jr, and his 'brother John Borden'; it mentions an inheritance from the Fowle family of Cranbrook, Kent, and land he had bought of Edward Williams in 1696, and was proved on 9 May 1706 (WO-0183). Compiled Borden genealogies make him a son of Richard and Joan (Fowle) Borden of Portsmouth, Rhode Island, and so a brother of Benjamin Borden who married Abigail Grover (WO-0177). If his son Francis was the father of John Borden Sr, he is a great-great-grandfather of Lydia Borden (G8), wife of Amos Wolcott.
