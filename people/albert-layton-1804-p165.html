What the records say

Albert Layton married Catharine Wagoner in Monmouth County on 5 September 1828 [S-0225]. By 1840 he headed a household of four sons and two daughters in Fulton County, Illinois, among New Jersey neighbours named Hand and Soper [S-0231]. In October 1850 he was a 45-year-old New Jersey-born farmer in Fulton County, Illinois, with Catharine (50) and children Aaron (21), William (17?) and Reuben (14), born in New Jersey, and Lydia (12) and Mary E. (8), born in Illinois [S-0227]. An online tree makes an Albert Layton (1808-1883) a son of Thomas Layton (1783-1838) and Rebecca Ann Matthews [S-0226]. In June 1880 Albert, 75, a broom-maker, and Catharine, 79, both born in New Jersey of New Jersey-born parents, lived in Timber Township, Peoria County [S-0230].
