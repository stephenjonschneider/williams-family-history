What the records say

The settler's (G12) will of 17–18 Aug 1702 names Andrew among his sons (L-0022, L-0413). Stillwell records that Andrew Layton entered an earmark at Middletown on 11 Mar 1725/6 (L-0222). By deed poll of 23 Oct 1761 Andrew Layton of Middletown, signing by mark, and his wife Rebecah conveyed to Andrew Layton Junior of Middletown the tract by the mill run, with the settler's Poplar land, the 100 a. by Grover and Throckmorton and the Wakecake meadows (L-0127). That younger Andrew's will of 1800 names his brothers James, Samuel and John (L-0332). An Andrew Layton was sued by Gawen Drummond over cattle at the July Term 1763, but the record does not show which Andrew (L-0334).
