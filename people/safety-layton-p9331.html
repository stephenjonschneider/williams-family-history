What the records say

On 20 Nov 1818 Safety and his wife Ann mortgaged "the farm that was John Layton's, father of the said Safety" to Thomas Henderson (S-0054); Henderson's executors later swore his bond of that day was "for land sold to him" (S-0121). The farm was levied on in 1836 and again in 1837, when Safety and John S. Layton were living on it together (S-0123, S-0124). In May 1838 "Safety Layton of the township of Howell" took on a judgment "against his son John Layton" (S-0125). He was dead by 23 Mar 1841, when James M. Hartshorne took administration (S-0069); in 1842 the farm went to John S. Layton (S-0070, S-0072). In Aug 1846 his widow Anne and his sons Jonathan and Grandin were before the Orphans Court (S-0128).
