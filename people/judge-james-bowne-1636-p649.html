What the records say

James Bowne (G12?) was baptized at Salem in 1636: the First Church register lists "James son of William Bound" (BG-0742). He married Mary Stout (G12?) at Gravesend on 26 Dec 1665 (BG-0290, BG-0299). He drew Lot No. 2 on Portland Point in 1667 (BG-0170) and recorded his earmark there in 1669 (BG-0161). A 1675 warrant gave land "in right of him selfe & his wife Mary Stout" (BG-0367), and his 1677 patent covered 212 acres at Middletown (BG-0372); he bought 80 acres from Richard Hartshorne in 1678 (BG-0341) and had 480 acres surveyed at Crosswicks in 1688 (BG-0385). His earmark was "recorded to his son Samuel Bowne" in 1679 (BG-0304). He was dead by Feb 1696: his inventory was taken by his nephew John Bowne and his brother-in-law Jonathan Stout, and administration went to his son James, with his brother Andrew as bondsman (BG-0294, BG-0019).
