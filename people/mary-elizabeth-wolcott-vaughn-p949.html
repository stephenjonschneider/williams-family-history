What the records say

Her father Henry H. Wolcott's (G7) will names his daughter Mary E. Vaughn (WO-0060), and in 1876 Mary E. Vaughn, wife of Clark Vaughn, gave the executors a receipt for her $200 legacy (WO-0142). A county biography of her brother Charles lists her among Henry H.'s children (WO-0081). She was living in 1908, when her brother Amos's obituary names Mrs Clark Vaughn among his sisters (WO-0076).
