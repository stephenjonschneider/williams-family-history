What the records say

Mordecai Grover is known from a single appearance as a witness at Middletown in 1705. Stillwell calls him "probably a son of Safety Grover" and adds that "for the present I know nothing more concerning him"; he noted that the name Mordecai was found in Monmouth only among the Gibbons and Lincoln families, and wondered whether Safety Grover's wife was a Gibbons or a Lincoln (BG-0531, BG-0738). Dornan's notes also list a Mordecai among Safety's six children (BG-0653). He is not in James Grover's 1780 will and probably died young or without issue.
