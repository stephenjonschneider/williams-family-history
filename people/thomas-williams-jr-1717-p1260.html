What the records say

On 28 January 1740/41 "Thomas Williams Jun'r", with John Williams and Daniel Estill as sureties, was licensed by the Court of Quarter Sessions to keep a public house and inn at his home in Upper Freehold, on condition that he allow no gaming and keep good order (W-0060). The "Jun." is the only hint that his father was a Thomas — perhaps Thomas Williams (G11) of Freehold. A Thomas Williams sued debtors in 1742, 1755 and 1767 (W-0095, W-0071, W-0072), but these may be other men of the same name. No record names his wife or children.
