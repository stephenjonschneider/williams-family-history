What the records say

The 1900 census lists Clarence W., 14, born May 1886, as a son in George W. and Armenia Newman's Wall household (N-0280). Find a Grave links a Clarence Wakely Newman as George Wakefield Newman's child (N-0281). In 1930 Clarence W. Newman, 43, a dredger, owned a $2,000 home in Wall with his wife Sarah B. and children Kenneth B., Barbara C. and Harriet A., a few doors from his brother Archibald (N-0284).
