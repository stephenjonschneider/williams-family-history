BETHEL · 1851–1956

Every name in one place

Look at the Howell maps of 1851 and 1873 around Bethel (Southard) and you can read this whole history in the names on the farms: Layton, Williams, Newman, Cooper and Matthews, all within a mile or two of each other along the North Branch of the Metedeconk. Thomas Edward Williams was born there in 1862. His uncle Henry (W. H. H.) Williams, the youngest of Daniel and Elizabeth's nine children, kept a store at Southard and in 1878 bought a farm on Kent Road thought to be the northern part of the old 1788 tract. When Henry moved his store to Asbury Park in 1894, Thomas took over his farm. Henry was buried in the family plot at Bethel in 1917. Thomas later moved to Point Pleasant, where his children, including Harry Franklin Williams, raised the next generation. Stout, Bowne, Grover, Layton, Brewer, Newman, Wolcott and more all come together in the Williams family.
