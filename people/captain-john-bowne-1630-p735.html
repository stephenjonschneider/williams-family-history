What the records say

Capt. John Bowne was one of the Monmouth patentees of 1665 (BG-0133) and a deputy to the Assembly in 1668 and 1675 (BG-0134, BG-0490). A warrant of 1676 laid out 900 acres for him, surveyed and patented in 1677 as 941 acres in seventeen parcels (BG-0368, BG-0004, BG-0371). He sat as a justice from 1676 (BG-0178, BG-0181), presided over the Court of Sessions in 1683 (BG-0174) and was commissioned major in Dec 1683 (BG-0493). He died in Jan 1683/4; his inventory came to £427 3s 6d, and administration went to his widow Lydia, with his son John as bondsman (BG-0293, BG-0281). In 1686 Lydia and her son John divided the estate by articles of agreement (BG-0507).
