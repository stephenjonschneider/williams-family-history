What the records say

Jone was the wife of Peter Woolcott at Burlington by early 1686, when the Friends' meeting arranged care for Peter 'and his wife, being poor' (WO-0097). The birth register names her as the mother of Benjamin, born 20 May 1687 (WO-0089). As 'Joane Woolcot of Burlington' she married William Worth of East New Jersey at the Burlington meeting house in April 1695, and later lived at Shrewsbury; the witnesses included Lippincotts and a 'Benj: Woolcot' (WO-0138). A memorial index gives her burial in the Friends burial ground at Shrewsbury and a death of 15 September 1722 (WO-0137); a family tree gives 15 September 1720 (WO-0004).
