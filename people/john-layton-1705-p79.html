What the records say

On 25 July 1751 Joseph Throckmorton Jr sold John Layton a right to 50 a., which was surveyed that August on the west side of Marsh's Bog in Shrewsbury (L-0051, L-0052). In 1752 William Brinley sold John Layton of Shrewsbury, yeoman, 20 a. of bog meadow, and a further survey was laid near the forks of Yellow Brook (L-0202, L-0053). A John Layton of Freehold, carpenter, was sued by Richard Stillwell in 1755, perhaps this man (L-0335). His will of 1 Mar 1761, proved 13 May 1761, names his sons Safety, Andrew, John, William (G9) and Thomas and his daughters Catharine, Hester, Hannah and Ann; his wife was living but is not named (L-0054). His son John mortgaged his 101 a. share of the land in 1766 (L-0223), and a Catherine Layton, probably his widow, was a Shrewsbury householder in 1779–81 (L-0345).
