Documented (father) · Probable (mother) — Richard Stout (G13?, P61) & Penelope (G13?, P99): his father's will of 1703 names his son Richard, and records of 1687–89 call him "Junior" (BG-0310, BG-0446).

What the records say

Richard Stout Junior bought land on Swimming River from the sachems in 1676 and had it patented beside James Grover's that June (BG-0428, BG-0432). A further 120 acres were surveyed in 1686 and patented in 1687 to "Richard Stoutt Junior of Middletown" (BG-0438, BG-0446). He sued Samuel Leonard in 1687 (BG-0104) and bounded the Pesequenecque Brook tracts in 1689–1709 (BG-0105). After his father's will of 1703 (BG-0310) he appears as "Richard Stout Senior of Shrewsbury", selling with his son Robert in 1705 and with his wife Mary in 1709 and 1713 (BG-0232, BG-0235, BG-0237), and giving land to his son Joseph in 1734 (BG-0229). Whether the 1734 deed is his or a son's is not settled.
