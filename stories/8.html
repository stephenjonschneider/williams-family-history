MIERY BRANCH, SOUTHARD · 1788–1850

Into the Metedeconk woods

On 22 May 1788 Daniel Williams had land surveyed on the Miery Branch of the Metedeconk. The survey begins at the house he was then building, on ground where the Route 9 Walmart in Howell stands today. The next day Joseph Layton, a son of Safety Layton, had 30 acres surveyed beside him, and Daniel is named in that survey too. Joseph's uncle was William John Layton, and in 1798 Daniel and William witnessed a survey together at Miery Run while Joseph and his cousin John carried the chain, nearly thirty years before the two families married. His wife was Mary Brewer. Daniel died intestate by December 1801, and his inventory still held Mary's £50 legacy from her father. His children divided the land in 1832. Where Daniel and Mary lie is not certain: very probably at Adelphia, though in 1864 one acre of the partition land was set aside for a graveyard, which may have been a family burying ground.

His son Daniel married Elizabeth Layton in 1826. She was a granddaughter of William Layton of the North Branch, so the Layton and Williams lands now ran together. Daniel's sister Catharine married a Layton cousin too. Daniel died in 1843 and was buried at Adelphia, near his grandmother Ellenor Neeltje (Cooper) Brewer. The Orphans Court of 1849 names his children, among them Daniel D..
