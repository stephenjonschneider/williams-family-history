What the records say

Donato 'Harry' DiCorcia was born in Italy on 4 October 1882 and came to Jersey City as a teenager about 1898 with his mother's household; his father Francesco, a stone mason, had come earlier [W-0547, W-0549]. By 1910 he was a railroad gateman living with his parents and siblings on Second Street; he married Mary soon after and raised eight children there, working as a Pennsylvania Railroad brakeman [W-0540, W-0549]. About 1923 the family moved to Point Pleasant Beach [W-0541]. In 1942 he worked on the Inland Waterway bridges at West Point Pleasant, later becoming a Route 35 bridge operator [W-0550, W-0543]. Widowed in 1945, he lived with his daughter Florence's family until his death on 27 December 1950 [W-0553, W-0543].
